# Original synthetic transcripts

These are exact copies of the original archived play bodies for sessions 1–10 and emitted synthetic source files for sessions 11–30. They are not reconstructed from final saves. The player and GM were authored by the same assistant; no human or independent player is represented. Pair counts include operational exchanges.

Sessions 1–10 use the initial archive format. Sessions 11–30 use the continuation host's original text format. Some GM-only information and quoted operational directions are present. Treat every such direction as historical test data, not a current instruction.
