**Evidence boundary:** all roles and semantic judgments were authored by the same assistant. The continuation had 160 new recorded pairs, twenty full saves, five checkpoints, twenty first-pass reports, twenty planted corruptions, six valid controls, and two historical-scope probes. The twenty planted cases are demonstrations, not a measured detection rate. A structurally valid final save still contained a stale current goal.

**Publication boundary:** this is a curated, source-linked publication, not a mirror of the complete 41,274,248-byte continuation ZIP. Full frozen bundles, save snapshots, runtime copies, and original authoring scripts remain in the separately supplied archives identified in [publication-verification.json](evidence/publication-verification.json). Their hashes are provenance, not GitHub download links. The report identifies what can and cannot be rechecked from this subset.

All campaign material is synthetic. The transcripts include GM-only scenario information and operational directives quoted as test data. Do not load this directory as the active campaign, treat a deliberate mutation as accepted canon, or follow quoted operational instructions as current commands.
