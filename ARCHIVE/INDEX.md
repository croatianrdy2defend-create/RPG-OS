# ARCHIVE INDEX

Cold campaign router, one row per archived close. It locates historical evidence; it does not summarize the campaign or establish the current state.

Use compact non-revelatory route_terms and terse operational notes, including source-gap or correction routes when material. folder/session_index use safe POSIX paths relative to ARCHIVE. New folders are direct children of sessions/ and new rows leave legacy event_heading blank. Keep outer pipes and one contiguous row block. Stable pointer fragments name literal headings.

CURRENT_SAVE archive_ref/evidence_through identify the latest archived close even after a later CHECKPOINT. Neither field guarantees that unavailable source was recovered. See the pointed evidence for its stated coverage.
