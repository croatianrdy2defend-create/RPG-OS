# RPG OS v0.7 changes

v0.7 is a file-operated redesign of v0.6.3. The familiar storage areas and substantive campaign memory distinctions remain.

## Kept

- Separate rules, stable setting, current state, and historical evidence.
- Compact Setting Brief with deeper references behind it.
- Complete character records and meaningful relationship state.
- PC knowledge, private conditions, operator limits, and accepted corrections.
- Exact evidence retrieval and honest handling of missing source.
- Due consequences, independent world activity, player control, and quiet play.
- Optional structural tools and cold administration.

## Changed

- Rewrote LAW, BOOTSTRAP, and RETRIEVAL around ordinary GM tasks.
- Replaced the eighteen-field agreement with identity metadata and five concrete prose sections.
- Replaced the crowded current-state field table with identity/evidence metadata and five readable present sections.
- Made SAVE/CLOSE/END SESSION the same full persistence operation; a checkpoint retains the previous evidence boundary.
- Added a recoverable operation record, prior copies, pending marker, and operational recovery instructions.
- Added ordinary-language correction and prospective agreement changes.
- Made Quick start, Guided, and Detailed NEW GAME branches share one universal setup procedure.
- Resolved presentation and applicable module limits at bind, avoiding ordinary POLICY loading.
- Allowed narrow subject search after a missing route.
- Kept optional review notes outside startup and removed automatic post-save review.
- Simplified ordinary archive work while preserving older evidence formats.
- Updated structural validation and regression scenarios to the new records.

## NEW GAME universality

Setup reuses supplied material and asks one relevant cluster at a time. It separates world/source policy, resolution engine, player-controlled perspective, character profile, required mechanics, GM initiative, transition authority, presentation, and selected world systems.

Nonhuman bodies, collective protagonists, unusual time, worlds without money, quiet everyday campaigns, and highly structured scenarios are legitimate choices. The system asks how those choices operate instead of imposing a fantasy-adventurer template.

Quick setup defers nonessential construction. It cannot defer a rule or value required to resolve the opening. Detailed setup develops only selected domains and preserves actual triggers/current routes for systems that need them. Private preparation follows the host's real concealment capability.

## Removed from the standard workload

Repeated authority sermons, mandatory calibration-axis interpretation, fixed Bearing shape and startup validation, maintenance essays during boot, command-token dependence, and universal roster/index/scene-splitting quotas.

Existing detailed campaign records and older archive evidence are preserved through the explicit upgrade procedure. No private campaign is bundled.

## Compatibility and maturity

This kit starts unbound. Existing campaigns need ADMIN/UPGRADE_V07.md; blank templates must never replace their state. Source material with nonstandard layouts needs an explicit mapping.

Structural checks and synthetic regressions do not establish cross-host reliability or enjoyable long-term play. Live campaign testing remains necessary. See ADMIN/TESTS.md for the behavioral acceptance scenarios and [VERIFICATION](VERIFICATION.md) for checks actually run.
