# INSTANCE/PEOPLE

Copy-on-write person overlays. Empty until a person is promoted or a `## NOW` / `## PC` fact must persist.
Before an overlay or accepted post-save transition exists, and only while no declared causal transition has occurred or is due, continuity may derive from one specifically named MODULE as-of-T0 `## NOW` / `## PC` snapshot when causally required. PLAY keeps later unsaved change in chat RAM. At the next explicit CHECKPOINT/CLOSE, materialize the complete as-of-now mutable surface (`NOW`, `PC`, and only affected current PRIVATE state) here and apply every accepted change once; keep stable CANON and unrelated PRIVATE lore in MODULE. After that, MODULE remains T0 history and the overlay owns current state.

For an emergent promoted person with no MODULE record, the INSTANCE record also contains only their established minimal `## CANON`. Assign one stable portable `person_id`, use it for the filename and CAST_STATUS mapping, and never invent missing identity or promote a transient extra.
Do not store progression under MODULES/.
An overlay may store a direct pointer to its authoritative archive scene heading. MESSAGES_LEDGER and RELATION_LEDGER pointers themselves always target a source file under a declared ARCHIVE close folder; existing legacy transcript sources there remain valid.
