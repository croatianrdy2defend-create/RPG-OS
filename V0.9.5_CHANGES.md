# v0.9.5 — Session preparation, closure and feedback

Released 2026-09-10. Experimental protocol release; the verification record distinguishes structural checks from observed GM behavior.

## A connected campaign between sessions

The new [session procedure](ADMIN/SESSION.md) requires relevant causal preparation at begin/resume and consolidation plus feedback at an explicit ending. The GM recovers actual prior choices, consequences, current commitments and applicable player feedback, then prepares a useful continuation within the accepted authority. Finished undertakings remain finished; quiet continuations need no replacement threat or prescribed escalation.

Feedback is a required GM duty. The player can answer or decline, and immediate stopping can save with unfinished feedback identified. Actual feedback and its permitted treatment carry forward. Silence is not an answer; a preference does not rewrite fictional events or silently change the accepted agreement.

## Small, explicit record ownership

- Optional administrative `Session continuity` in CURRENT_SAVE preserves the play-session identity, phase, opening basis and feedback status, with real pending items. The existing 13 metadata fields and five fictional sections remain unchanged. A clean bind has `none`; absent legacy coverage remains unknown.
- On-demand `INSTANCE/PREP.md` holds derivative, source-linked causal context and conditional preparation. It is created only by useful authorized post-bind persistence, never shipped empty or copied from module T0. Required facts, feedback and mechanical application records survive its absence.
- Existing archive bodies may retain narrowly labelled OOC session operational evidence: actual feedback, its disposition and relevant boundary adjudication. Private preparation and unrelated administrative/provider chatter remain excluded. Archive folder/index grammar and the four commit kinds are unchanged.

## Save, end and continue

SAVE and bare CLOSE preserve complete current state and available accepted evidence. Explicit END SESSION invokes consolidation, feedback and genuinely due engine procedures, then the existing full save. Natural wording determines whether the player is ending play. “Save and end” invokes the wrapper once.

A logical play session can span chats, saves, scenes and handovers. Those events do not automatically end it, advance fictional time or reset encounter state. An engine declares actual beginning/end procedures or explicitly supplies none; applications use distinct session/boundary/rule references at their effect owners. Repeated ends, zero adjudications, save retries and late feedback cannot create another award opportunity. Freeform adds no automatic XP, rest reset or downtime allotment.

## Setup, compatibility and verification

NEW GAME at Quick, Guided and Detailed depth, existing-module LOAD, module contracts, QUICKSTART and the worked end-session example all include the routine. Responsibilities fit the existing five agreement sections and five named clauses, without extra mandatory setup interviews. Source-governed neutrality remains required before worldbuilding; worked examples do not supply generation weights or campaign facts.

Upgrade through [the compatible-campaign procedure](ADMIN/UPGRADE_V08.md). Preserve current records, source fidelity, existing people, selected methods and exact evidence. Adopt new session terms prospectively in the existing agreement where needed; program installation alone creates no session, award, PREP, fictional development or new authority. Never install the blank public INSTANCE over a played campaign.

Focused validator, packaging and handover regressions cover the new structural surfaces. [Session cases](ADMIN/TEST_SESSION.md) describe connected behavioral checks; their actual execution and limits are recorded in [VERIFICATION](VERIFICATION.md). Passing schema checks does not prove causal reasoning, feedback quality or long-campaign reliability.

This public package remains unbound and includes only Freeform as a selectable engine. No private campaign module, character, feedback, history, preparation or source artwork is distributed.
