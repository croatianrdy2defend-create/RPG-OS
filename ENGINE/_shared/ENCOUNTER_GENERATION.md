# Optional encounter generation — direction, then concrete state

## Initial generation

Use only when selected by the bound engine and accepted agreement. This helper is not a default random policy or a reception table. Paths beginning with OS or INSTANCE are from the campaign root. `OS/AGENT_STATE.md#Initial Encounter Baseline` owns the five fields, activation, decision scope and lifecycle; this procedure supplies eligible initial determinations.

## Establish scope before input

Recover surviving state and the relevant world sources for actual nature, control, capacities, senses and circumstances. Locate the decision owner and local discretion. Group membership, intelligence or power implies no shared mind, unlimited knowledge or human psychology. Use established personality and history without generating a compulsory biography.

Before dependent individual behavior, complete **Condition and mode; Priorities and constraints; Perception and appraisal; Attraction; Engagement stance**. Each needs a concrete value, supported inapplicability, or a precise source/perception limitation with its ending condition. Unknown is not neutral, absent or inapplicable. Retrieve missing required authority; fill eligible gaps rather than defaulting them to neutrality.

Identify which of condition, initial interpersonal appraisal and overall attraction remains eligible for generation. Fix the unresolved facet and its scope before drawing; do not prepare six named states or a custom outcome table. Established facts remain binding. A known injury, for example, is not rerolled; an eligible emotional aspect may vary only if that capacity and aspect were identified beforehand. Do not choose a different aspect after seeing the result.

Use only information actually received through established channels. The PC's upcoming action is not already perceived. Unperceived player wishes, preferred outcomes and intended narrative roles cannot select inputs or dictate concrete values.

## Direction, then concrete determination

Use two independent actual d6 for each eligible facet and sum that pair:

| 2d6 total | Direction and intensity |
|---|---|
| 2–3 | Strongly negative |
| 4–5 | Negative |
| 6–8 | Neutral or mixed |
| 9–10 | Positive |
| 11–12 | Strongly positive |

These are accepted game conventions, not population probabilities or a moral scale. The pair supplies direction and intensity; **the GM then establishes the concrete current value, before choosing dependent behavior**. A direction label alone does not complete a field. Use a short, meaningful determination within source constraints, without an extra intensity roll, invented past cause or new capacity. Strong means a marked degree within the eligible facet's supported range, not an extreme event, permanent trait, diagnosis, compulsion or removal of boundaries. Neutral/mixed must mean a concrete absence of directional pull or supported countervailing considerations, not an empty placeholder. The middle band occurs in 16 of 36 equally likely pairs; each strong band in 3 of 36. Do not draw a uniform total from 2 to 12 or redistribute bands to force variety.

The direction has a different reference for each eligible facet:

- **Condition:** favorable or adverse to the entity's own current functioning or activity, not to the PC's success. Apply only the previously identified physical, emotional or operational aspect. Preserve other known condition facts; a positive direction grants no healing, ability or equipment.
- **Initial interpersonal appraisal:** favorable or unfavorable evaluation of the PC based on supported perception and information. A favorable direction does not prove identity or trustworthiness and does not establish acceptance, cooperation or automatic action. Actual sensory access and factual knowledge are never rolled by this table.
- **Overall attraction:** personal pull toward or aversion from the PC within the supported attraction domain. Neutral/mixed can establish no particular pull or a concrete ambivalence consistent with known constraints. It supplies no general approval, hostility, availability, commitment or participation.

Do not select a response first and construct its inputs afterward. Keep independently obtained directions and their concrete values even when they complicate the encounter; do not normalize, reinterpret or reroll them to secure cooperation or resistance.

## Complete the other fields from actual mechanisms

**Priorities and constraints** have no moral polarity roll. Establish a concrete current aim, drive, directive or operative process and its practical limits from sources and current circumstances, independently of the PC's hoped-for result. State what it pursues, preserves or avoids and how that can matter now. An activity label alone is insufficient where competing needs or commitments remain relevant. Eligible detail may be authored within the accepted scope; it creates no unsupported order, history or ability.

**Perception and appraisal** retains actual detection, recognition, information and limitations. A nonsocial entity uses its sourced classification, control and response mechanisms rather than an interpersonal liking roll. A chemical responder, animal, controlled unit or powerful entity receives only capacities established for it; none of those labels proves a particular mind, incapacity or sensory reach. Deterministic mechanisms need no invented private preference. Predation, chemical response and target selection are not personal attraction by analogy.

**Attraction** must receive its applicable concrete value at first sufficient perception. Being busy, on duty or partnered does not remove eligibility. Physical/aesthetic, sexual and romantic detail can be resolved later when needed within surviving constraints; deferring subtypes cannot leave overall attraction blank. A subtype-specific exclusion applies only to that subtype. Supported inapplicability requires no draw; an unread source or unknown capacity cannot be relabelled inapplicable.

Absence of sufficient information delays only the determinations it cannot support. Complete them before first use when that information arrives, without rerolling existing fields.

**Engagement stance** or the appropriate nonsocial response policy is derived from the complete basis and situation, without a stance or willingness draw. It informs the pending decision; the bound engine resolves that decision once. Different priorities, appraisal and attraction can coexist without forcing a uniform response.

## Input and continuity

A wholly eligible baseline uses **at most three 2d6 determinations, six independent faces total**: one pair for condition, one for initial interpersonal appraisal and one for overall attraction. Fix pair-to-facet assignment before input. Fixed facts, supported inapplicability and precise perception limitations reduce input. Priorities, factual perception and engagement add no draws. Use an available real randomizer or actual player-supplied faces, independently with replacement and in one batch where practical. Never share a face between facets/entities or invent reported rolls. If real input is unavailable, obtain it or agree an available alternative.

This helper adds no ongoing reception or attraction-change roll, modifies no reserved PC decision and replaces no applicable sourced nonsocial mechanism. Follow `OS/AGENT_STATE.md` for coordinated groups, active-state reuse, affected-field changes, genuine deactivation and later incidental activation. Retain concise concrete values, limitations and actual input/method references needed for continuity. NOW's **Active encounter state** owns saved temporary state; existing person/system owners retain consequential facts. No visible field checklist, private scratch file, per-turn write or automatic checkpoint is introduced; unsaved state remains subject to actual host retention.
