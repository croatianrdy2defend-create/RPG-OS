# SETUP — Optional world and campaign depth

Use only for domains selected during NEW GAME. Reuse the accepted premise and draft agreement. This guide develops material; it does not introduce another mandatory interview or runtime policy.

## Choose the useful depth

Offer a short relevant selection: world/ordinary life; places and exploration; people/institutions; hidden truth and mysteries; changing conditions; relationships; resources/logistics; rule calibration; or a custom domain. Work one selected area at a time. Quick, Guided, and Detailed can all stop with the runnable minimum.

World detail, dynamics, accounting precision, and mechanical complexity are independent. Detailed means depth inside a selection, not every domain, massive rosters, or a larger resident prompt. Keep one cohesive body until independent retrieval would benefit from a split.

## The actual agreement

Return material decisions to the five-section run proposal: Campaign promise, Player control, GM initiative, Time and transitions, Presentation. Include what kinds of developments may be authored, applicable conditions/limits, what must proceed once established, and any routine/cut grants. Do not infer a grant from a genre or label.

POLICY's Voice and Campaign defaults are reusable setup proposals. Source expectations also belong there for future setup, but all accepted constraints affecting PLAY are copied into the Campaign promise/brief or a real source-authority body reached by an exact contract/descriptor route; the brief advertises only domain/trigger/capability. Voice/guidance goes into Presentation; accepted stronger module limits into SAFETY with their origin. POLICY never remains the sole home of a runtime fidelity rule. Detailed world machinery stays behind capabilities.

## World and ordinary life

Select only useful dimensions: reality, places, bodies/material accommodation, inhabitants/personhood, technology/powers, travel/communication, history, society, institutions, ecology, or other relevant domains. No human, individual, terrestrial, monetary, social, or calendar model is presumed.

For relevant domains establish:

- Public baseline and provenance: supplied/accepted invention, historical or external source and edition/continuity, or hybrid.
- What is ordinary, rare, localized, restricted, variable, or impossible; qualitative descriptions usually suffice.
- Concrete appearance/operation and everyday consequences where these matter.
- The few generic model assumptions that would distort this world.
- What the GM may invent from the brief, what existing exact authority must be retrieved, and what is deliberately unfixed.

A category does not automatically determine individual conduct. Explicitly established species, collective, social, or physical facts may constrain it; do not replace an accepted alien reality with universal human individualism or substitute stereotypes for source facts. Separate claims, beliefs, private truth, and observations.

Produce the public Setting Brief under the three required headings. Keep named cast, mutable state, detailed lore, private truth, preparation, and future events cold. Available depth names real capabilities and when their detail is needed, not a compulsory reading list. Local variation need not display every world category.

## Optional systems: enough to operate and resume

For each selected mutable system, retain only relevant information in its authoritative body:

- A stable safe id, purpose, visibility, and accepted as-of-T0 state.
- Whether it is definition-only, dormant, or live at the opening.
- Its actual change conditions, what does not change it, and any threshold/resolution behavior.
- One selected route for later current state: a NOW entry/shard, a person/PC record, or a named CURRENT_SAVE section.
- Any non-revelatory condition/route cue needed in Active processes to make a live private check discoverable after boot.
- Dependencies and a clear update order where one cause affects several systems.
- Minimal provenance: source or accepted setup basis; later current records identify the save/accepted transition establishing their values.

Do not require every field for static lore. A setup summary is not durable authority: operational declarations must live in the selected body. Detailed current values have one owner. A MODULE T0 snapshot stays historical after a later transition; point to the selected INSTANCE route rather than merging incompatible moments.

### Places, exploration, and prepared situations

An authored location may fix its contents and triggers. An entered room's established contents apply without a separate invitation to introduce them. Unexplored content can remain open where the accepted source/fairness policy permits.

Prepared possibilities are candidates, not live events. They may describe contact conditions, stakes, constraints, and dependencies. Keep them separate from established hidden facts and live processes. Fix an uncertainty before resolving an attempt when the selected experience depends on an independently existing answer; do not choose the hidden answer after seeing the player's guess or result.

### Hidden information

Choose an honest mode for each needed private domain:

- **Reviewed:** the operator sees and accepts exact private facts.
- **Constrained private generation:** the operator accepts domain, scope, exclusions, and authority before generation; write privately only if the host actually permits content to remain unexposed.
- **Unfixed:** preserve the unknown under accepted generation constraints until legitimately established.

A filename, heading, index gist, or watch cue can spoil a secret; use non-revelatory ids/routes. Workspace privacy is not encryption and cannot conceal files from their owner. Do not promise hidden tools, another agent, or guaranteed secrecy. If the host echoes writes, offer reviewed or unfixed preparation. No private envelope supplies undelegated PC history, decisions, commitments, or mechanical values.

### People, factions, and institutions

Develop actors whose recurrence or independent action matters: motives, resources, constraints, knowledge/misbeliefs, relationships, characteristic behavior, and the next applicable action or condition. Include distinctive dialogue/portrayal cues where useful. Do not prebuild everyone.

An established actor can act when due without waiting for PC attention. An invented actor's existence is not a requirement to feature them. A scheduled battle can start without assuming the PC joins it. Use one stable person id for routing and later INSTANCE overlays.

### Conditions, phases, clocks, and fronts

Use the structure appropriate to the campaign: physical condition, phase, clock, event order, schedule, or another model. Explain actual triggers and consequences. Elapsed time advances a track only when time is a declared cause; retrieval and session endings do not advance it by themselves.

A campaign may explicitly authorize chapter progression or transitions. State those grants in the agreement instead of treating all phases as either compulsory plot or forbidden direction. Preserve the distinction between a world transition and the PC's response.

### Relationships and social structures

Track established connections at useful granularity: trust, obligation, access, conflict, care, affiliation, attraction, or other relevant aspects. Preserve consequential promises and short representative episodes/quotes rather than reducing every relationship to a score.

Do not presume human pair-bonding, exclusivity, romance, family structure, or one social model. An NPC can initiate and respond independently. The player controls undelegated PC participation and commitments; a declared act may be resolved without asking permission for the same act again.

### Resources, logistics, and time

Choose abstract, selective, or exact accounting in named domains. A campaign need not use currency, inventories, biological needs, or dates. Use its actual units or meaningful event ordering.

Distinguish owned, stored, carried, equipped, available, spent, owed, and estimated where consequential. Keep each exact total in one current authority and link to it; do not maintain competing totals in a sheet and save. Routine compression includes only declared or explicitly delegated conduct.

### Rules and calibration

Use RULES_HOOKS for compatible campaign clarifications. A genuine change to engine resolution or character rules requires a distinct engine id through ADD_ENGINE; recheck character/opening requirements if the binding changes. Later table rulings belong in CORRECTIONS.

A new engine needs known procedures and inputs. Do not use world detail, agreement language, or model confidence to supply missing rules. Keep commercial-source adapters compact and source expectations honest.

## Finish the selected work

Before returning to NEW GAME:

1. Check the selected domains fit each other and the accepted agreement. Resolve only meaningful contradictions.
2. Identify opening dependencies: mechanics, current values, live conditions, and player-owned decisions. Ensure only relevant cues/routes enter T0's five readable sections.
3. For interacting systems, walk one representative cause through its consequences exactly once, with a selected owner for each changed value.
4. Rehearse one meaningful retrieval for each complex capability. Repair routes that require reading unrelated private material or discovering an unknown hierarchy.
5. Return the brief, real capability bodies, essential routes, source/private envelopes, and meaningful unknowns for the single NEW GAME review.

Do not persist here. The shared LOAD bind commits the accepted work with recovery protection. Omitted areas remain honestly absent or open.
