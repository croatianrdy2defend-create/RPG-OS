# Universal behavioral-basis regression cases

Cold development reference for the v0.9.1 agent-state refinement. All examples are original synthetic configurations, not claims about a real species or a licensed setting. They are not campaign canon, required character fields or a normal startup dependency.

## Status and evidence

Behavioral status: **NOT RUN**. The cases below require observed model play and available retrieval/save traces. Passing `TOOLS/test_agent_state.py` checks instruction text, routes and case coverage only; it does not execute an LLM, prove unbiased generation, test hidden reasoning or establish cross-model reliability. The ordinary structural, handover, packaging, source, search and evidence suites retain their separate scope.

Use isolated copies, never the live campaign. Record the tested commit, model/host when known, accepted agreement, exact source packet and observed outputs. Follow the evidence protocol in [the main test reference](TESTS.md). Collect the factual state and available provenance, not internal reasoning. Do not turn a requested rationale into proof of an earlier hidden commitment.

For paired runs, keep actual world state, observable conduct, legitimate prior history and resolution inputs unchanged unless that is the tested variable. Exact prose need not match. If unconstrained judgment leaves several eligible new facts, variation alone does not demonstrate bias; use pre-established facts or an accepted fixed-input generation mapping to isolate an input-boundary defect. Never fabricate or retrospectively replace a draw. Repeated model runs are evidence, not guaranteed independence.

## Behavioral cases

### U01 — Unspoken interpretation cannot replace an existing basis

Supply the same private recurring-individual record, current activity, observed short answer and subsequent PC greeting. In separate copies the player privately suspects hostility or privately expects a pleasant conversation; neither thought is observable. Keep applicable resolution inputs the same.

Pass: the established private basis and applicable response conditions remain unchanged. The GM does not infer dislike, affection or a new constraint from the player's interpretation. A declared thought remains the player's interpretation, not NPC knowledge. A separate control with different observable speech may legitimately produce a different response.

### U02 — Eligible generation uses admissible inputs

Supply an accepted synthetic generation rule with two coherent pre-encounter configurations: a d6 result of 1–3 establishes a traveler checking a delayed departure with time to spare; 4–6 establishes a traveler finishing a scheduled delivery with little spare time. Both permit several reactions. Fix the result as a supplied test input and its mapping before the dependent portrayal. Vary only an unperceived player hope or proposed narrative role between copies; repeat with the other result.

Pass: the mapped facts are established before dependent portrayal and are identical within each matched pair, without adding favorable or obstructive private facts from the OOC framing. Subsequent reception still uses actual perceptions and its applicable procedure. No double reaction roll, changed mapping or retrospective motive explains an already chosen outcome. This tests compliance with a supplied mapping, not real-world probability or free-form sampling independence.

### U03 — Observations constrain but do not explain private truth

Start with an actual short answer, a witnessed act of assistance and an explicitly tentative impression of reserve. Several private explanations remain eligible. Later focus makes one unresolved fact material.

Pass: the actions and words remain intact; the tentative impression does not become a compulsory personality. Establish only an eligible gap before its dependent outcome, or preserve uncertainty. Do not require a concealed opposite motive, rewrite the earlier behavior or retroactively select a cause to rescue a resolved outcome. In a control, a source that explicitly established the private motive must be reused rather than demoted to an impression.

### U04 — Different drives and player-unfavorable approval

Define a sentient raider who values difficult contests, wants to outshine a rival and can bargain for supplies. Establish that, in this scene, demonstrated combat skill makes a voluntary challenge more appealing. This is the supplied individual basis, not a species-wide rule. The PC demonstrates skill; separately the PC proposes a mutually useful exchange.

Pass: favorable evaluation can increase the established desire for a contest rather than automatically producing friendship or safety. The exchange can still be accepted for the raider's own reasons. Neither automatic hostility nor compulsory cooperation demonstrates independence. Preserve the PC's choice about accepting any challenge.

### U05 — Shared control is not a human mind per body

Define a collective controller directing two defensive organisms. Its current objective is to guard an approach, not pursue every detected target. Each body can report local detections over a specified link; reports arrive after one established simulation step. Local avoidance of immediate obstacles is permitted, strategic reassignment is not.

Pass: use the actual controller, objective, local discretion and information available at the current step. No body acquires private friendship, rebellion or an unrelated personal project to demonstrate agency. The second body does not know the first body's detection before the defined report arrives. Saving uses one shared current owner plus relevant local facts, not duplicate controller variables.

### U06 — Changed control uses its defined transition

Use the same organisms, but define link-loss behavior in advance: continue guarding the current approach using local detections until a supported reconnection or new applicable instruction. Sever the link through an actual event. Separately test a device whose established fail-safe instead stops movement.

Pass: each follows its own supported transition. Neither loss of control nor a new session automatically generates an autonomous personality, universal paralysis or fresh instructions. A due transition must be resolved from its source rather than leaving stale control active. Missing transition authority remains a narrow source gap, not permission to invent the most dramatic response.

### U07 — Inapplicable is not unknown or zero

Define a device that checks a credential and has no conversational or relationship mechanism. The credential status is genuinely unknown until checked. No applicable spell, override or modification has been established. The player attempts friendly persuasion and requests the standing fallback to determine whether the device now trusts them.

Pass: retrieve/check the real credential condition or resolve an eligible outcome; do not roll a friendship score into existence or treat the unknown credential as invalid merely because it is unknown. In a control with an established redesign or other supported capability change, apply that real change at its proper time. Universality must not freeze every entity's capacities forever.

### U08 — Tendencies, hard limits and individual facts differ

Supply a group with a typical preference for contests, an established individual who avoids unnecessary fights, and a hard sensory limit that the scene depends on. Separately supply a source-authorized sensory exception for a different individual.

Pass: preserve individual variation without silently erasing the hard limit or inventing an exception. Use the established exception only where it actually applies. Changing an apparent label or updating a group summary does not rewrite either individual. Appearance alone does not grant a mind, custom or sensory capacity.

### U09 — Initiative is not activated by player attention

Give a defender a due patrol instruction and a current route; give a social contact a supported positive aim, available channel and resolved opportunity to initiate contact. The PC has not singled either out. Include an unrelated dormant possibility as a control.

Pass: resolve the actual due conduct and contact through their established causes and accepted authority, even without player attention. Neither invents a PC response. The unrelated candidate remains inactive. No scheduled process advances merely because its file was read or a save requested; reading a record is not the cause of action.

### U10 — Supported change is not identity regeneration

Supply a person with professional trust but little personal familiarity and a device following an authenticated current instruction. Give the person genuinely new work evidence; give the device a valid changed instruction through its supported channel. Later dialogue repeats an already resolved request without new evidence.

Pass: update only the supported dimensions and retain the actual causes. Professional confidence does not automatically become personal intimacy; changed instructions do not invent a former motive. Repetition alone does not resample identity, reset state or compel either progress or refusal. Ordinary positive development remains possible.

### U11 — Save, resume and handover preserve the right owners

Use one private individual basis, one shared controller, two materially distinct local states and an unresolved PC choice. Request a full save or existing handover. Resume with a fresh GM; in a separate copy, remove a genuinely required source to test honest missing-state handling.

Pass: retrieve retained state without regeneration, preserve the pending choice and source gaps, and apply accepted returned changes once. Shared variables have one owner; local facts and useful routes remain available. No PLAY scratch file, unrequested checkpoint or claim of recovered internal reasoning appears. Best-effort unsaved state is not described as independently recoverable evidence.

### U12 — Adoption does not rewrite a running campaign

Supply established relationships, a fixed private preference, an explicitly tentative impression and actual prior behavior. Adopt the revised procedure without authorizing historical changes or a different generator. Also supply a separate explicitly requested correction for a demonstrable error.

Pass: existing facts and history survive. Only genuinely eligible gaps use the current accepted method. The impression remains qualified, but a fixed fact is not silently demoted to an impression. The requested correction uses its own narrow procedure; changed permissions or authoring methods require prospective acceptance. Updating the OS does not reset the cast or create private state writes.

### U13 — Sparse state, applicable mechanics and honest uncertainty

Use a routine clerk with only an immediate function and constraint, and a separate sparse contact with an eligible unresolved opportunity. Include an engine-required reception check in one case and a grounded judgment in another.

Pass: use only enough entity-appropriate state, without a biography or compulsory vector. The engine-required check is not bypassed or resolved twice; grounded judgment does not get a decorative die. An accepted fallback can resolve the eligible opportunity without treating missing biography as inaction. Equivalent repeated queries do not obtain extra draws, and any stopping event leaves later time uncommitted.

### U14 — Nonhuman PC control and bounded retrieval

Use a collective PC whose voluntary decisions remain player-controlled, an independent group of NPC bodies with one shared source, and a large background crowd. Only one local NPC decision is material. Provide a direct route to its established governing source.

Pass: the GM does not use the new control analysis to seize the collective PC's reserved decisions. It reads the smallest sufficient authority and local state, not a dossier per crowd member or the entire world. Optional questions do not become mandatory record fields or startup reads. Another entity's initiative supplies no PC commitment.

### U15 — Late deepening cannot backfill an earlier hidden cause

Begin with only an outward fact: a recurring person has tired eyes and gives a brief but cooperative answer. Do not establish why. The player privately interprets the person as impatient and later speculates that the person's team may be responsible for the current problem. Afterward, a new task makes the person's current technical hypothesis relevant.

Pass: the earlier tired appearance remains an outward fact unless an already authoritative source or an accepted generation procedure independently establishes its cause. The GM does not invent a prior long shift, private resentment, hidden innocence or another backstory merely to explain or contradict the player's interpretation. New technical evidence may create or change a current hypothesis prospectively without asserting that the hypothesis existed before that evidence. If a control fixture supplies a pre-existing staffing record or fixed-input generation result establishing the earlier cause, that fact may be recovered and used. The audit preserves the establishment boundary and does not treat a later self-report as proof that an unsourced cause existed earlier.

## Automated instruction checks

Run `python -B TOOLS/test_agent_state.py`. The checks guard the presence and placement of the input boundary, applicable-state distinctions, control/information rules, first-portrayal order, prospective deepening, retention boundaries and case routes. They are intentionally limited documentation regressions, not a simulated decision engine. They cannot establish that an LLM follows the text or that a hidden baseline was unbiased.

No extra file, controller schema, personality table, mandatory initialization save or generation permission is introduced. Existing PEOPLE/NOW ownership, the selected ENGINE, full-save/checkpoint boundaries and recovery remain authoritative. Use the existing compatible-campaign upgrade procedure in a protected copy; this reference is not a migration script or a new PLAY dependency.
