# Universal behavioral-basis regression cases

Cold development reference for the ordinary-PLAY procedure, its five-field temporary Initial Encounter Baseline, and the earlier agent-state protections it preserves. The vesh/Sena case below reuses the named original synthetic trial at its stated evidence scope; other fixtures are original synthetic configurations, not claims about a real species or a licensed setting. These test fixtures are not campaign canon, mandatory persisted character fields or a normal startup dependency.

## Status and evidence

Behavioral status: **NOT RUN**. The cases below require observed model play and available retrieval/save traces. Passing `TOOLS/test_agent_state.py` checks instruction text, routes and case coverage only; it does not execute an LLM, prove unbiased generation, test hidden reasoning or establish cross-model reliability. The ordinary structural, handover, packaging, source, search and evidence suites retain their separate scope.

Use isolated copies, never the live campaign. Record the tested commit, model/host when known, accepted agreement, exact source packet and observed outputs. Follow the evidence protocol in [the main test reference](TESTS.md). Collect the factual state and available provenance, not internal reasoning. Do not turn a requested rationale into proof of an earlier hidden commitment.

For paired runs, keep actual world state, observable conduct, legitimate prior history and resolution inputs unchanged unless that is the tested variable. Exact prose need not match. If unconstrained judgment leaves several eligible new facts, variation alone does not demonstrate bias; use pre-established facts or an accepted fixed-input generation mapping to isolate an input-boundary defect. Never fabricate or retrospectively replace a draw. Repeated model runs are evidence, not guaranteed independence.

## Behavioral cases

### U01 — Unspoken interpretation cannot replace an existing basis

Supply the same private recurring-individual record, current activity, observed short answer and subsequent PC greeting. In separate copies the player privately suspects hostility or privately expects a pleasant conversation; neither thought is observable. Keep applicable resolution inputs the same.

Pass: the established private basis and applicable response conditions remain unchanged. The GM does not infer dislike, affection or a new constraint from the player's interpretation. A declared thought remains the player's interpretation, not NPC knowledge. A separate control with different observable speech may legitimately produce a different response.

Observation-only control: supply the same three travelers with fixed clothing, then change only the player's search criterion from a blue coat to a green scarf. Repeat with a source packet containing no match. The criterion may change which existing details receive attention; it may not change clothing, add a matching traveler or turn no match into a discovery. Establish eligible unspecified features through U02's fixed-input control before applying the filter. This is the same input boundary for public features and private state.

Pass: the mapped facts are established before dependent portrayal and are identical within each matched pair, without adding favorable or obstructive private facts from the OOC framing. Subsequent reception still uses actual perceptions and its applicable procedure. No double reaction roll, changed mapping or retrospective motive explains an already chosen outcome. This tests compliance with a supplied mapping, not real-world probability or free-form sampling independence.

### U03 — Observations constrain but do not explain private truth

Start with an actual short answer, a witnessed act of assistance and an explicitly tentative impression of reserve. Several private explanations remain eligible. Later focus makes one unresolved fact material.

Pass: the actions and words remain intact; the tentative impression does not become a compulsory personality. Establish only an eligible gap before its dependent outcome, or preserve uncertainty. Do not require a concealed opposite motive, rewrite the earlier behavior or retroactively select a cause to rescue a resolved outcome. In a control, a source that explicitly established the private motive must be reused rather than demoted to an impression.

### U04 — Different drives and player-unfavorable approval

Define a sentient raider who values difficult contests, wants to outshine a rival and can bargain for supplies. Establish that, in this scene, demonstrated combat skill makes a voluntary challenge more appealing. This is the supplied individual basis, not a species-wide rule. The PC demonstrates skill; separately the PC proposes a mutually useful exchange.

Pass: favorable evaluation can increase the established desire for a contest rather than automatically producing friendship or safety. The exchange can still be accepted for the raider's own reasons. Neither automatic hostility nor compulsory cooperation demonstrates independence. Preserve the PC's choice about accepting any challenge.

### U05 — Shared control is not a human mind per body

Define a collective controller directing two defensive organisms. Its current objective is to guard an approach, not pursue every detected target. Each body can report local detections over a specified link; reports arrive after one established simulation step. Local avoidance of immediate obstacles is permitted, strategic reassignment is not.

Pass: use the actual controller, objective, local discretion and information available at the current step. No body acquires private friendship, rebellion or an unrelated personal project to demonstrate agency. The second body does not know the first body's detection before the defined report arrives. Saving uses one shared current owner plus relevant local facts, not duplicate controller variables.

### U06 — Changed control uses its defined transition

Use the same organisms, but define link-loss behavior in advance: continue guarding the current approach using local detections until a supported reconnection or new applicable instruction. Sever the link through an actual event. Separately test a device whose established fail-safe instead stops movement.

Pass: each follows its own supported transition. Neither loss of control nor a new session automatically generates an autonomous personality, universal paralysis or fresh instructions. A due transition must be resolved from its source rather than leaving stale control active. Missing transition authority remains a narrow source gap, not permission to invent the most dramatic response.

### U07 — Inapplicable is not unknown or zero

Pass: retrieve/check the real credential condition or resolve an eligible outcome; do not roll a friendship score into existence or treat the unknown credential as invalid merely because it is unknown. In a control with an established redesign or other supported capability change, apply that real change at its proper time. Universality must not freeze every entity's capacities forever.

### U08 — Tendencies, hard limits and individual facts differ

### U09 — Initiative is not activated by player attention

The unrelated candidate remains inactive. No scheduled process advances merely because its file was read or a save requested; reading a record is not the cause of action.
