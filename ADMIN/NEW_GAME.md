# SETUP — New Game

Use after a request to create a campaign. This is a draft conversation, not PLAY. Reuse supplied facts; ask one small cluster of useful questions at a time. The goal is a playable campaign the player understands and accepts, not a completed questionnaire.

## Protect existing work

Resolve `RECOVERY/ACTIVE.md`, if present, through `ADMIN/RECOVERY.md`. Read CURRENT_SAVE and make one bounded INSTANCE/ARCHIVE inventory. A bound run, prior campaign evidence, non-template overlays/registers, or unexplained files require a separate clean kit. Never reset or inherit an old campaign to make NEW GAME succeed. Bound upgrades use `ADMIN/UPGRADE_V07.md`.

Engine/module ids use ASCII letters, digits, `.`, `_`, `-`, begin with a letter or digit, and contain no separators or traversal. Propose a safe id from the accepted name; the player need not invent technical identifiers. Check the exact module destination is absent and both permitted engine paths for collisions before planning writes. Never overwrite an installed module through NEW GAME.

## Choose a useful pace

Offer these once unless the request already establishes the desired depth:

- **Quick start:** propose a coherent minimal campaign, PC, and opening from the supplied idea. Ask only what materially blocks it, then show the compact proposal.
- **Guided:** develop the premise, agreement, world, character, and opening through short adaptive clusters.
- **Detailed:** let the player select domains and develop those in stages. Stop or defer optional depth whenever requested.

These are setup preferences, not runtime modes. World depth, character detail, bookkeeping precision, and mechanics are independent. A quick campaign may use a complete tactical sheet; a detailed world may use Freeform. Do not repeat already answered depth questions.

## Build the playable minimum

Follow the player's starting point rather than a fixed question order. Establish the following with as few exchanges as practical. Open `ADMIN/CAMPAIGN_BUILD.md` for selected world/system depth and `ADMIN/CHARACTER_BUILD.md` for character work.

1. **The experience.** Establish premise, played perspective, important activities, material inclusions/exclusions, and desired style. Sandboxes, assigned missions, keyed exploration, cinematic chapters, domestic life, and combinations can all fit. Labels can explain a proposal; the actual agreement controls play.
2. **Limits and presentation.** Invite operator limits early, before sensitive drafting. Record accepted hard-no/fade sentences under SAFETY's canonical labels; no personal explanation is required. If no voice was supplied, propose clear concrete prose, direct NPC dialogue, enough orientation to act, and concise responses; accept or revise that proposal with the agreement. Propose dialogue/narration balance, guidance, and status display only at useful granularity.
3. **Rules.** Use the named engine or offer installed choices and the option to supply another. Inspect canonical `ENGINE/<id>.md` and `ENGINE/<id>/ENGINE.md` entries with matching id and `class: engine`; aliases are not engines. State resolution, values needed now, and creation support: `self-contained`, `operator-values-required`, or `no-mechanical-sheet`. Follow `ADMIN/ADD_ENGINE.md` for a new adapter. Never silently substitute Freeform or invent missing rules.
4. **The world.** Establish original, historical, established-source, or hybrid basis; opening scope; and relevant reality, inhabitants, ordinary distribution/variation, and model-default pitfalls. Do not assume human bodies, individual personhood, Earth time, money, a calendar, gender categories, romance, or fantasy cosmology. Omit irrelevant dimensions. Record source era/edition/continuity and priority where needed, plus exact fidelity, guided invention, and deliberate unknowns. A setting title is not evidence of a specific fact.
5. **The PC.** Establish the controlled perspective, essential concept, accepted starting facts, and engine minimum. An individual, nonhuman being, collective, organization, or another viewpoint can work if decision ownership and mechanical representation are clear. Optional history and sheet fields may remain unspecified. A character description does not grant GM control.
6. **Authority and continuation.** Propose what the GM may introduce, which established processes it must advance, any delegated routine PC conduct, and reserved meaningful decisions. State what a declared activity lets the GM carry through, when it should return control, and allowed compression/cuts. A due world event does not imply PC participation. Permission is never an incident quota.
7. **The opening.** Propose one oriented playable situation: place, relevant time/order, perceivable circumstances, accepted prior commitments, ongoing activity, and the next material decision. Quiet is valid; a requested active premise should offer something usable to engage with. Do not invent PC history, allegiance, feelings, first action, or consent. Show proposed prior PC facts separately for acceptance. Opening mechanics must be available before bind.

Use supplied or accessible source references and label gaps honestly. Request the needed fact/procedure, preserve an unknown, or propose a clear divergence. Copy every source/fidelity constraint that affects PLAY into the agreement/brief, or a real source-authority body with an exact contract/descriptor route. The brief advertises its domain, trigger, and capability only; never leave an operative constraint solely in cold POLICY. Adapters contain compact procedures and field requirements, not reconstructed books or bulk copyrighted tables. For random mechanics, establish an available real randomizer or player-provided results; no mandatory helper script is assumed.

## Prepare one reviewable proposal

Show actual proposed content concisely:

- The agreement's **Campaign promise; Player control; GM initiative; Time and transitions; Presentation** sections. Include source expectations and concrete grants. Do not use retired axis, creative-mandate, or review-mode fields.
- The Setting Brief's **World identity; What is ordinary; Available depth**. Distinguish brief-guided portrayal from existing cold authority.
- The PC, mechanical readiness/deferrals, opening, and meaningful unknowns.
- Selected optional systems and their initial status/current routes; any private-authoring envelope and real host disclosure limitations.
- The small file manifest and accepted limits, distinguishing operator and module origins.

Read `MODULES/_CONTRACT.md`, `ENGINE/_CONTRACT.md`, and `INSTANCE/_SCHEMA.md` before finalizing. POLICY retains `## Voice` and `## Campaign defaults` as setup proposals. Accepted presentation goes in the run contract; accepted stronger module limits go in SAFETY. POLICY is cold during PLAY.

Ordinary explicit acceptance of the displayed proposal suffices; no magic phrase is required. Re-present only material revisions or unresolved choices. A request to begin can accept a complete unambiguous proposal, but cannot fill missing grants or values. Unaccepted alternatives remain drafts.

## Commit and hand over

Use the shared bind procedure in `ADMIN/LOAD.md` with this accepted draft and its new-file manifest. It protects all writes through recovery, verifies the results, and publishes CURRENT_SAVE last. Do not write persistent campaign files during the open interview or split module creation and bind into unprotected operations.

Create only MODULE, SETTING_BRIEF, POLICY, the PC baseline/bundle, T0_SAVE, and selected real capability bodies. No mandatory roster, empty encyclopedia, or opening interpretation is needed.

After verification, report campaign/save identity, meaningful omissions, and how to resume from `OS/AGENTS.md`. Recommend a fresh PLAY chat so rejected drafts are cold. If the player continues here, perform the normal boot reads and use only accepted bound material.
