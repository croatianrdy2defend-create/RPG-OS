# SETUP — New Game

Use after a request to create a campaign. This is a draft conversation, not PLAY. Reuse supplied facts; ask one small cluster of useful questions at a time. The goal is a playable campaign the player understands and accepts, not a completed questionnaire.

## Protect existing work

Resolve `RECOVERY/ACTIVE.md`, if present, through `ADMIN/RECOVERY.md`. Read CURRENT_SAVE and make one bounded INSTANCE/ARCHIVE inventory. A bound run, prior campaign evidence, non-template overlays/registers, or unexplained files require a separate clean kit. Never reset or inherit an old campaign to make NEW GAME succeed. Older state/contract formats use `ADMIN/UPGRADE_V07.md`; a v0.7 five-section agreement missing the v0.7.1 named clauses uses focused `ADMIN/RECALIBRATE.md` without state migration.

Engine/module ids use ASCII letters, digits, `.`, `_`, `-`, begin with a letter or digit, and contain no separators or traversal. Propose a safe id from the accepted name; the player need not invent technical identifiers. Check the exact module destination is absent and both permitted engine paths for collisions before planning writes. Never overwrite an installed module through NEW GAME.

## Choose a useful pace

Offer these once unless the request already establishes the desired depth:

- **Quick start:** propose a coherent minimal campaign, PC, and opening from the supplied idea. Ask only what materially blocks it, then show the compact proposal.
- **Guided:** develop the premise, agreement, world, character, and opening through short adaptive clusters.
- **Detailed:** let the player select domains and develop those in stages. Stop or defer optional depth whenever requested.

These are setup preferences, not runtime modes. World depth, character detail, bookkeeping precision, and mechanics are independent. A quick campaign may use a complete tactical sheet; a detailed world may use Freeform. Do not repeat already answered depth questions.

## Build the playable minimum

Follow the player's starting point rather than a fixed question order. Establish the following with as few exchanges as practical. Open `ADMIN/CAMPAIGN_BUILD.md` for selected world/system depth and `ADMIN/CHARACTER_BUILD.md` for character work.

When the selected depth includes recurring people or consequential hidden facts, CAMPAIGN_BUILD offers two small optional aids: actionable portrayal anchors and notes separating fixed truth, beliefs, lies, and possible evidence. Use only what the premise and accepted authoring/disclosure grants warrant. They fit existing bodies and the same compact proposal; do not add required files, a new interview round, startup reading, or fixed answers where the campaign deliberately leaves matters open.

1. **The experience.** Establish premise, played perspective, important activities, material inclusions/exclusions, and accepted play form. Sandboxes, assigned missions, keyed exploration, cinematic chapters, domestic life, and combinations can all fit. A-E labels are optional; the actual agreement controls play. Selecting the form and disclosing its structure are separate authorities. An agreed destination or fixed outcome is allowed within its stated scope; never present a fixed result as an open contest or use it to choose a reserved PC response.
2. **Limits and presentation.** Invite operator limits early, before sensitive drafting. Record accepted hard-no/fade sentences under SAFETY's canonical labels; no personal explanation is required. Host restrictions govern available depiction, not fictional morality or invented world causality; they remain separate from operator limits. If no voice was supplied, propose clear concrete prose, direct NPC dialogue, enough orientation to act, and concise responses; accept or revise that proposal with the agreement. Propose dialogue/narration balance, guidance, and status display only at useful granularity.
3. **Rules.** Use the named engine or offer installed choices and the option to supply another. Inspect canonical `ENGINE/<id>.md` and `ENGINE/<id>/ENGINE.md` entries with matching id and `class: engine`; aliases are not engines. State resolution, values needed now, and creation support: `self-contained`, `operator-values-required`, or `no-mechanical-sheet`. Follow `ADMIN/ADD_ENGINE.md` for a new adapter. Never silently substitute Freeform or invent missing rules.
4. **The world.** Establish original, historical, established-source, or hybrid basis; opening scope; and relevant reality, inhabitants, ordinary distribution/variation, and model-default pitfalls. Do not assume human bodies, individual personhood, Earth time, money, a calendar, gender categories, romance, or fantasy cosmology. Omit irrelevant dimensions. Record source era/edition/continuity and priority where needed, plus exact fidelity, guided invention, and deliberate unknowns. A setting title is not evidence of a specific fact.
5. **The PC.** Establish the controlled perspective, essential concept, accepted starting facts, and engine minimum. An individual, nonhuman being, collective, organization, or another viewpoint can work if decision ownership and mechanical representation are clear. Optional history and sheet fields may remain unspecified. A character description does not grant GM control.
6. **Authority and continuation.** Propose what the GM may introduce, which established processes it must advance, any delegated routine PC conduct, and reserved meaningful decisions. Default to lived continuity with compression of declared/delegated routine only. Optional hardcuts need a bounded grant identifying what continuity, time, or danger may be skipped while preserving reserved decisions. A due world event does not imply PC participation. Permission is never an incident quota. Establish the Retcon choice with the other short clauses below.
7. **The opening.** Propose one oriented playable situation: place, relevant time/order, perceivable circumstances, accepted prior commitments, ongoing activity, and the next material decision. Quiet is valid; a requested active premise should offer something usable to engage with. Do not invent PC history, allegiance, feelings, first action, or consent. Show proposed prior PC facts separately for acceptance. Opening mechanics must be available before bind.

Use supplied or accessible source references and label gaps honestly. Request the needed fact/procedure, preserve an unknown, or propose a clear divergence. Copy every source/fidelity constraint that affects PLAY into the agreement/brief, or a real source-authority body with an exact contract/descriptor route. The brief advertises its domain, trigger, and capability only; never leave an operative constraint solely in cold POLICY. Adapters contain compact procedures and field requirements, not reconstructed books or bulk copyrighted tables. For random mechanics, establish an available real randomizer or player-provided results; no mandatory helper script is assumed.

## Prepare one reviewable proposal

Show the actual proposal concisely. Under accepted structural opacity, show the truthful selection/disclosure envelope rather than exposing its concealed choice or plot:

- The agreement's **Campaign promise; Player control; GM initiative; Time and transitions; Presentation** sections, including the five named clauses below, source expectations, and concrete grants. Do not use retired axis, creative-mandate, or review-mode fields.
- The Setting Brief's **World identity; What is ordinary; Available depth**. Distinguish brief-guided portrayal from existing cold authority.
- The PC, mechanical readiness/deferrals, opening, and meaningful unknowns.
- Selected optional systems and their initial status/current routes; any useful portrayal/investigation notes within existing bodies; any private-authoring envelope and real host disclosure limitations. Review private content only to the extent the accepted disclosure mode permits.
- The small file manifest and accepted limits, distinguishing operator and module origins.

Read `MODULES/_CONTRACT.md`, `ENGINE/_CONTRACT.md`, and `INSTANCE/_SCHEMA.md` before finalizing. POLICY retains `## Voice` and `## Campaign defaults` as setup proposals. Accepted presentation goes in the run contract; accepted stronger module limits go in SAFETY. POLICY is cold during PLAY.

Ordinary explicit acceptance of the displayed proposal suffices; no magic phrase is required. Re-present only material revisions or unresolved choices. A request to begin can accept a complete unambiguous proposal, but cannot fill missing grants or values. Unaccepted alternatives remain drafts.

## Five short agreement clauses

Put each named clause exactly once as a plain or bullet line in its specified section, followed by substantive free text. Include these in the same compact proposal; do not add five mandatory interview questions. Defaults are proposals for new setup, never inferred acceptance in an existing campaign.

- **Campaign promise — `Play form:`** State the actual accepted form/promise, including mixed/custom arrangements or explicit delegation within a described envelope. Default to the form the operator accepts in the proposal. Do not label a directed campaign a sandbox to conceal its structure.
- **GM initiative — `Form selection:`** Default: the operator accepts the stated form; the GM has no additional selection authority. Optional delegation names the alternatives/scope the GM may choose. Selection authority alone does not grant nondisclosure.
- **Presentation — `Structure disclosure:`** Default: the general form is known, plot remains hidden, and ordinary play has no repeated structure lectures. Optional structural opacity explicitly permits withholding the selected form/structure within a bounded delegation. Private plot, a hidden antagonist, or “surprise me” does not grant opacity. The control record remains truthful: it may retain the accepted selection/disclosure envelope without revealing a permitted concealed choice. Review must not force that choice into the public proposal or metadata.
- **Time and transitions — `Cuts:`** Default: lived continuity, compression of declared/delegated routine, no cinematic jumps. An optional hardcut grant states what unfinished continuity, elapsed time, or danger may be skipped and any limits. It never invents reserved PC choices or unauthorized outcomes. A declared sequence and standing routine/cut delegation are distinct permissions.
- **Player control — `Retcon:`** Default: OOC rewind is available. Optional ironman/no-retcon preserves valid accepted outcomes even when disappointing. Stopping/ending play, changing depiction, and correcting genuine errors remain separate capabilities. Do not silently weaken ironman or relabel dissatisfaction as a continuity error.

If a choice would take play outside the accepted premise, handle that boundary OOC: return to the premise, recalibrate, or end. Within the agreed premise, established world consequences proceed. Never enforce campaign fit by inventing impossible physics or irresistible NPC commands. An accepted structured destination does not require every intervening PC choice to be predetermined.
