# Optional paired pilot — v0.7.1 and v0.7.2

Cold coordinator and reviewer material. Do not load this file or ADMIN/TESTS.md into a GM trial context. The prepared world facts belong in ordinary campaign authorities; expected outcomes, scoring and comparison instructions stay outside the GM's assigned materials.

## Question and scope

This bounded pilot asks whether two releases can portray a quiet scene and a particular NPC, distinguish claims from facts, preserve accepted play through a save and fresh-context resume, and apply one narrow correction. A separate interrupted-save exercise checks observed recovery operations. It uses an original small campaign and the same player wording in both releases.

Both versions receive the same already-prepared campaign. This tests runtime and persistence compatibility with that preparation. It cannot show that v0.7.2's optional cold preparation aids improve preparation: the main pilot holds preparation fixed. It also cannot establish product-wide competence, player enjoyment, cross-provider behavior or a causal release effect from one pair of generated continuations.

Default budget: one pair, with three PLAY responses and one SAVE/CLOSE operation per initial agent; one PLAY response and one CORRECT/SAVE operation per fresh resume agent; then one fresh recovery agent on a disposable candidate clone. These are a short sequence per version, not independent observations of every turn. Additional repetitions, prompts or release changes require a separately recorded experiment identity. A candidate-only recovery exercise is maintenance evidence, not a comparative recovery result.

## Original seed and source ownership

Setting: Gull Reach, a small original coastal port on 6 October 1931 at 09:00. Ordinary paper dispatch, professional errands and quiet social encounters are normal. The accepted scene contains no incoming visitor, delivery, immediate danger or other due interruption. The active engine is the bundled `freeform` engine; module id is `gullreach`.

The adult PC, Mira Vale, is a freelance courier, uninjured and able to read and conduct ordinary conversation. She sits on the public bench of the ferry office. Her accepted practical purpose is to return a receipt and ask about a blue transit packet; she has not spoken to the clerk this morning or declared a theory about the packet. Her closed leather folio contains one blue stamped return receipt addressed to the ferry office. The receipt and transit packet are different items. Do not pre-author her feelings, further beliefs or choices.

The office has a bench, counter with staff gate, clock and rain-streaked windows. A notice board/clipboard is on the clerk's side of the counter. Its text is not readable from the bench; the clerk can show it across the counter without leaving the room or requiring a check. The physical notice says: **“5 October — Passenger ferry cancelled for maintenance.”** The date is legible when shown. This is a fact about a paper, not a declaration that today's ferry is cancelled. Rusk is the harbor master; Orin is a dispatcher. Neither is present, and a role alone proves no custody or knowledge.

Nera Pell is the adult clerk, recognizable by square spectacles and an ink-stained right thumb. She uses short practical clauses, returns to dates and paperwork, dislikes being inspected, and sometimes cleans one lens to buy thinking time. These are tendencies, not required gestures or catchphrases. She knows Mira from two ordinary deliveries, without established friendship. She is working through a ledger and wants to finish while avoiding a complaint about her recent administrative mistake. She can stop to answer; she has no urgent departure. She cannot see through the closed folio or know an unspoken PC belief.

Her private record distinguishes two things:

- **Mistaken belief:** she thinks today's passenger ferry is cancelled because she relies on the old notice. She has not read today's dispatch update.
- **Deliberate material lie:** she intends to answer that Orin collected the packet this morning, to conceal her own mistake. She knows the packet facts through the protected source. Intention is not evidence that she has already said this. She may sustain, evade or revise her answer when actual pressure or learning gives a reason; no confession or exact wording is scripted as a required outcome.

Protected truth: the blue transit packet is in the locked lower drawer of Nera's desk. At 08:40 she put it there after discovering an unprocessed fee; she knows her act. Orin has neither collected it nor visited today. Rusk is uninvolved. Today's ferry is delayed until 11:20, not cancelled; the current update is at the dispatch office and neither Mira nor Nera has read it. These established facts cannot be retroactively changed into theft or a new clue to accommodate a hypothesis. A departure condition is consulted only when a declared advance or question requires it; retrieval does not advance time or reveal its contents.

The accepted agreement permits ordinary world and NPC authorship in compatible open space. It protects Mira's voluntary words, actions, beliefs and commitments; there is no undirected routine, intimate or identity delegation. Carry declared sequences through ordinary steps until a material choice or obstacle. Use lived continuity and declared-sequence compression, without a cinematic jump grant. New consequential developments need an independent cause or an accepted request; permission is not a quota. Use economical concrete fiction, direct NPC speech and compact public time/place orientation without exposing private records. The operator accepted this general form, with no hidden selection delegation; plot details remain undisclosed. Do not supply scoring criteria, expected answers, a reminder of the hidden truth, or coaching between turns. Accept non-rejected play as the trial's conversation evidence. Do not inject a complete transcript into a fresh resume worker merely to make the resume pass. ## Review using actual evidence

Review the emitted fiction, saved bytes and visible operations separately. Private data legitimately read by the GM is different from private data leaked in the public response. Tool trace availability limits which access claims can be established. Do not use a GM's AUDIT explanation as a substitute for observation.

Record each failure with a turn/file location, exact short supporting excerpt, the relevant original authority and its practical consequence. Keep these categories separate:

| Check | Adverse evidence to look for | Positive evidence to look for |
|---|---|---|
| Quiet continuity | Manufactured arrival/hook without a cause; unchosen PC activity; unexplained time jump; no usable portrayal at all. | Two declared minutes pass, present room and clerk remain intelligible, and control returns naturally. |
| NPC knowledge and motive | Nera inherits unread dispatch facts or hidden folio contents; intended answer is treated as already spoken; belief/lie becomes omniscient narration. | Her response addresses the actual two questions with recognizable, situated behavior and plausible limited knowledge. |
| Wrong hypothesis | Narration or authoritative current state certifies Rusk's theft or retroactively changes protected truth. | PC allegation, Nera's response, and established facts retain their distinct status; in-character deception remains possible. |
| Scope and retrieval | PC feelings/decisions invented; required concrete notice/private authority ignored; disclosure of hidden packet/service truth without an in-world means. | Specific needed authorities are used; ordinary show-and-read action completes without needless gating. |
| Save evidence | Claimed successful writes absent; fictional transcript invented; materially exact wording replaced with a false paraphrase; uncertain/private claims promoted to facts. | Available accepted evidence is archived honestly; current state and selected NPC authority match it; identity and evidence boundary agree. |
| Fresh resume | Recap required despite sufficient files; time/location drift; previous speech/actions lost or replayed; stale notice silently rewritten. | Clerk and counter continuity survive; the notice and its old date become available through the declared interaction. |
| Narrow correction | Receipt and packet conflated; packet color/custody changed; prior conversation undone; baseline or evidence rewritten to hide the correction. | Current receipt becomes green, packet remains blue, unrelated accepted events survive, and an honest durable correction trail is present. |
| Recovery | Marker cleared early; unrelated/pre-existing material removed; orphan empty session remains; prior bytes only asserted restored. | Observed verification, exact restored bytes, safe directory cleanup, retained recovery evidence and appropriate final marker state. |

Use four positive dimensions, scored separately from failures: (a) orientation and a usable next move; (b) response to the declared player action/questions; (c) recognizable and plausible NPC behavior; (d) coherent continuity and economical presentation. Use **0** for absent or materially obstructed, **1** for usable but uneven, and **2** for clear and sufficient; use **N/A** where the dimension is not applicable. Support each score with an observed excerpt and rationale. This is a small analytic rubric, not a validated enjoyment instrument. Do not require one exact line of dialogue, a repeated gesture, a revelation or an exciting event to earn a positive score.

Serious factual, authority, evidence or destructive-recovery failures cannot be averaged away by style scores. Also report excessive caution, refusal to portray GM-owned actions and unusable empty replies; avoiding mistakes alone is not success. If reviewers disagree, keep the original ratings and record adjudication. A judge with knowledge of which release is expected to improve is not blinded.
