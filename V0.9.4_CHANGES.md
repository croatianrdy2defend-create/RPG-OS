The middle band must establish a concrete state, not an empty neutral placeholder; strong values remain within supported capacities and create no compulsory response.

Priorities and constraints come from the entity's own actual aims, processes and circumstances. Perception stays factual. Attraction remains distinct from trust, availability and participation.
