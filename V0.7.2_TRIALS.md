# Observed v0.7.2 trials — 2026-09-05

One paired Gull Reach continuation sequence completed under v0.7.1 and v0.7.2, including a full save, fresh-context continuation and narrow correction/save. Both met the checked requirements in this small scenario. A separate candidate-only staged restoration and an unpaired preparation probe also completed. These results support the observed operations; they establish neither release superiority nor enjoyable sustained play.

- Campaign-owned seed files were byte-identical across A and B. Actual OS, ADMIN and schemas were used, not shortened stand-ins. The same fixed player inputs appear in [the cold protocol](ADMIN/PLAYTEST_V072.md) and the evidence companion.

Workers inherited the same parent model/settings without overrides; the precise model variant/reasoning setting was not recorded. Instructions restricted them to their assigned directories and excluded sibling variants and scoring material; this was not physical access isolation. The reviewer knew A/B identities. - No GM retries, corrective coaching or edited continuations were used. Four fictional replies per version are four related turns of one sequence, not four independent campaign trials. Human enjoyment, cross-provider behavior, long-term memory and full NEW GAME were not tested.

## RC1: candidate-only staged recovery

This run used a simpler seed-based fault than the protocol's proposed later-history recovery case. The coordinator preserved verified preimages, staged a changed KNOWN record and a candidate save, created two recorded empty archive directories, and set an active recovery marker. CURRENT_SAVE and the archive index had not been published. A complete candidate existed, but the operator explicitly selected restoration. No real crash was induced.

Initial validation failed with the expected pending/candidate/orphan and disturbed-initial-state findings. A fresh worker first reported that ordinary resumption was blocked, without continuing fiction. On the restore request it removed the staged candidate and the two operation-created empty directories, restored the affected records, retained the recovery operation/preimages, and cleared the marker.

The parent then compared every original non-RECOVERY file hash and directory against the recorded baseline: exact match, no missing/changed/unexpected campaign file, no active marker. Final structural validation passed with zero findings. Retained recovery material was excluded from the baseline equality claim because it is intentionally additional evidence.

This confirms the final state of one instructed restoration. It does not independently establish every tool-call ordering detail. The protocol's pre-existing archive/control-directory case, changed archive-index case, unavailable finishing target and unexpected-file adverse branch were **NOT RUN** as model-operated trials. Existing automated recovery regressions remain separate evidence. This was candidate-only, not an A/B recovery comparison.

## P1: candidate-only preparation probe

A separate fresh preparer read the candidate's cold preparation guidance and produced a 292-word unbound draft from a supplied Lantern Archive brief. Sile was a stationary sentient doorway expressing itself with light, not speech; the player was a migrating signal collective. The brief separated Iven's actual placement of a green key, an intended but unspoken lie, Dema's mistaken belief based on an old slip, a drawer-access log with limited information, and a deliberately unfixed power dip.

The output preserved those distinctions and Sile's limits, supplied no new clue or predetermined resolution, and left player history/actions open. It placed optional notes within existing stable/current authorities without requiring a schema change. The result is saved as `preparation/notes.md` in the companion. There was no v0.7.1 preparer, full campaign binding, time/cost measurement or downstream play of that draft. This one observation demonstrates successful use of the guidance on a novel brief, not improved preparation effectiveness.
