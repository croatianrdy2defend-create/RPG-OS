# ADMIN — Optional campaign review

Explicit **REVIEW** requests only. No automatic review follows Save or END SESSION. Normal PLAY/boot does not load Bearing, and there is no review_mode contract gate.

The standard beginning/end routine and required GM feedback step belong to ADMIN/SESSION.md, not this optional review. Its source-linked preparation uses optional PREP; BEARING stays cold provisional review and cannot become the session-control, feedback or preparation authority.

A conversational review may remain in chat. If durable notes are requested, this operation writes only INSTANCE/BEARING.md under ADMIN/RECOVERY.md. Review is provisional interpretation, not current facts, agreed policy, preparation, or a new campaign obligation.

## Scope

Use the accepted agreement and current save, plus only the exact current records or historical sources needed for the question. Prior notes may be consulted as revisable interpretation. Never scan inactive preparation or the entire archive to find a direction.

For durable notes, identify campaign_id, base_save_id, base_contract_id, and actual sources reviewed. Base them on accepted persisted state. If relevant play remains unsaved, discuss it provisionally in chat or first perform a separately authorized full save; do not attribute unsaved developments to an older stored base. Missing evidence means not reviewed or unavailable, not that an event did not happen.

## Notes

Use whatever short headings the review needs; no fixed seven-section form is required. Keep distinctions visible where applicable:

- references to established developments;
- explicit PC declarations and explicit OOC preferences, distinguished from each other;
- observed conduct without inferred desire, consent, preferred plot, or a wish for more;
- provisional interpretations and open questions, each grounded in identified evidence.

A review may honestly conclude `no stable pattern yet`. Abstract directions are possibilities to consider, not queued scenes or instructions to realize them. Do not answer protected unknowns, tick systems, assign hidden historical causes, alter the agreement, or interpret participation as delegation.

When reviewing independent agents, compare portrayed responses and changes with actual current sources, knowledge and causes. Cooperation, refusal, initiative and indifference can all be appropriate; a rejection count is not independence, and never changing is not fidelity. Separate an unavailable source from a portrayal error. Optional natural-play incident notes follow `https://github.com/croatianrdy2defend-create/RPG-OS/blob/main/DOCS/ADMIN/PLAYTEST_V08.md`; they do not supply missing motives, retrospective probability tables or accepted world facts.

## Durable write

Prepare a compact candidate with the source bases and `status: provisional`. Compare it against the actual sources and current ids. Protect BEARING and the new candidate path using ADMIN/RECOVERY.md, then write and inspect INSTANCE/BEARING.candidate.md. Publish the checked notes to BEARING only; preserve the prior preimage and finish recovery.

Report the reviewed scope and that the notes are provisional. Do not expose private content unnecessarily. Later save/agreement changes make these notes older interpretation; they never override current authority. Missing or stale notes are nonfatal and are omitted from ordinary PLAY.

If a write fails, preserve the active recovery marker and restore or complete only the notes operation. Resolve that marker before PLAY; the prior campaign save and agreement are unaffected by review and require no invented change. Do not disguise review failure as save failure or claim that any provisional interpretation was accepted as canon.
