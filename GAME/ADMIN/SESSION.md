# ADMIN — Begin and end a play session

Use for beginning or resuming campaign play, an explicit session-ending request, or completing its feedback and wrap-up. This procedure connects relevant campaign history and preparation to ordinary PLAY. It does not replace LAW's Understand → Establish → Resolve → Portray cycle or optional REVIEW.

A play session can span several chats, saves, checkpoints, scenes, fictional days and GM handovers. Those boundaries do not themselves end a session. A clear request to end the session begins closure. Save and CLOSE remain full-persistence requests unless the actual wording also ends play. After an ended session, the next actual request to play begins the next session; readiness checks, feedback and maintenance do not.

Use CURRENT_SAVE's administrative Session continuity section for session identity, phase, opening basis and unfinished wrap-up. Actual mechanical effects and their application references remain with their selected current owners. INSTANCE/PREP.md is optional working preparation, never authority for these facts. Its absence is not evidence that a session is new or an award unpaid.

Respect pending RECOVERY and HANDOVER before performing source-side work. Do not start an operation merely because this file was read. A receiving GM follows SCENE_HANDOVER's separate authority and preserves the transferred session identity.

## Begin or resume

1. Complete normal BOOTSTRAP loading. Preserve the exact fictional moment, ongoing declaration, active encounter state and reserved decision. This procedure grants no PC action, time advance, process tick or new disclosure.
2. Recover Session continuity. Continue an active session under its existing id. If closing, address its actual outstanding step; an explicit request to resume PLAY may defer feedback or cancel an unfinished ending, according to what was already settled. Never silently reverse an applied mechanical result. If ended, preserve outstanding follow-ups and begin a new id only when actual PLAY resumes. A never-started bind has no played history. Legacy absence means no recorded identity: adopt prospectively from the known point and keep earlier coverage unknown rather than assigning imaginary past sessions or awards.
3. When a new logical session actually starts, retrieve and resolve only genuinely applicable engine beginning procedures, at the timing their actual rules require. Keep effects at their selected owners with a session/boundary/rule application reference; distinguish beginning from ending even when the rule name matches. Fresh chats, active-session resumes and handovers create no new beginning trigger. They may complete an expressly pending earlier procedure once. Prospective legacy identity adoption alone is not evidence that a beginning trigger occurred. Preserve missing sources and required choices precisely; do not resolve dependent PLAY without its required rule or input.
4. Check the exact optional path INSTANCE/PREP.md. Use source-linked notes that remain relevant and supported. Missing, foreign or obsolete preparation can be ignored or rebuilt from available authority. Do not bypass unsafe routes. Actual missing current facts and required rules remain source gaps.
5. Recover enough causal context to prepare the intended continuation: relevant prior decisions and outcomes, current commitments and actor/system circumstances, what completed or materially changed, and explicit player feedback still applicable. Use direct current and historical routes; extend retrieval only for an identified gap or contradiction. No routine full-archive, cast, lore or inactive-seed scan is required.
6. Revalidate conditional preparation against its real dependencies and accepted unsaved developments. Preserve supported local successes, changes in relationships and completed undertakings. Discard invalid possibilities rather than recreating their lost causes. A different save id alone does not invalidate all notes or prove that they were freshly reviewed.
7. Prepare only useful material within the accepted initiative and source grants. Distinguish due actions from possible opportunities. Establish any genuinely new consequential fact through LAW before treating it as real; place it at its usual working owner. A possibility must not decide an unknown past, fix a protected answer after a guess, or commit the PC. For a substantial finite activity, make its available scope, meaningful choices, applicable resolution and completion or withdrawal conditions usable. A quiet continuation and no new possibility are valid.
8. Resume ordinary PLAY. Offer a short public recap only when useful or requested, using the viewpoint's actual knowledge; never expose private causal notes or make the player read GM preparation. No additional “begin” confirmation is required after an actual request to play.

Preparation is working conversation until authorized persistence. Begin/resume does not create a file write or checkpoint. Keep the preparation's actual source bases and useful source pointers; do not restamp unchanged notes at every save.

## End the session

1. Honor the stopping request immediately: leave fiction at its actual boundary and preserve every pending player choice. If no actual play session has begun, preserve/save and stop without inventing a played boundary, retrospective, feedback obligation or award opportunity. Legacy history remains unknown where unsupported. Otherwise recover or assign the current prospective session id and mark closure in working conversation. An end request also authorizes the full save and completion of its feedback exchange. Do not ask again for permission to save.
2. Consolidate accepted developments and their causes, completed undertakings, continuing consequences, explicit commitments, and unresolved matters. Retrieve only needed sources. Do not fill historical gaps, invent offscreen events, expire active encounter state, or advance fictional time to manufacture a clean ending.
3. Include feedback as a required GM step. Reuse feedback already explicitly supplied for the session and invite any remaining feedback without repeating answered questions. Otherwise ask briefly what worked, what did not, and what the player wants changed or explored. Allow free-form answers and an explicit decline. Receive the reply before calling it received; silence is not approval, satisfaction or a decline. If persistence has not yet occurred, say briefly that the save remains pending. This is feedback, not an approval gate for the save.
4. Connect the actual feedback to a concrete prospective response where its scope is clear. Separate the player's statement, the GM's interpretation, the permitted adjustment, and any proposed change still requiring acceptance. A PC wish is not an OOC preference, and a preference is not world truth or NPC disposition. Directly implement clear instructions already within granted scope; use existing owners and procedures for actual agreement changes, operator limits or corrections. A material unresolved choice may remain pending without blocking unrelated work or persistence.
5. Retrieve and adjudicate only engine lifecycle procedures whose real triggers obtain. Check already applied results for this session and boundary before resolving anything. Record a result, including a resolved zero where appropriate, at its actual effect owner with the session/boundary/rule reference and source basis. Awarding resources is separate from choosing how to spend them. Missing rules, evidence or player selections remain precisely pending; do not invent a reset, purchase, award or training interval. Saving and repeating an end request create no additional opportunity.
6. Revise useful preparation: preserve the relevant causal context, reference the explicit feedback and its prospective treatment, identify continuing concerns, and remove fulfilled, invalid or superseded possibilities. Completed activities remain complete. Do not require a new threat or a longer plot to replace every finished concern.
7. Mark the play session ended, retaining any real outstanding administrative items with their session id and source route or exact unarchived content. An ended session may still have explicitly unfinished feedback or mechanical decisions; it does not certify that those were completed. Invoke CLOSE_CONTRACT's full save. It preserves the already established session work, normal current state, application references, available fictional evidence and narrowly allowed OOC operational evidence. Publish CURRENT_SAVE last through normal recovery.
8. Report the actual save result, relevant mechanical outcomes and any unfinished items concisely. Do not claim feedback was incorporated, an award settled or a record persisted unless that happened. No automatic REVIEW follows.

## Stopping before feedback or another required input

If the player requests an immediate stop, saving before further discussion, or cannot remain for the exchange, perform the full save with truthful pending/not-provided feedback and unresolved mechanics. Use pending when a reply or invitation remains outstanding; use not-provided when no answer was supplied at closure and no exchange remains open. Neither means declined, and both preserve any actual promised follow-up. A still-owed invitation remains an outstanding item for a suitable later opportunity. An immediate stop does not imply negative or positive feedback. Do not turn a feedback invitation into a demand to keep talking. The normal exchange may stay in conversation while awaiting a reply, but its pending status is durable only after an actual save; say when that save is still pending.

If available context or source access is at material risk, preserve the complete available state and evidence before waiting, under the existing end request. This is a full save of unfinished closure, not an automatically substituted checkpoint or a new persistence format. Continue the same closure when the reply arrives. The subsequent full save preserves the complete current state, applies only still-unsaved transitions, and archives only the newly available accepted evidence without reapplying earlier effects. Never claim that silence caused an unattended save.

Do not create RECOVERY/ACTIVE.md merely to wait for feedback. That marker is for a protected file mutation, and any live operation must be completed or recovered before waiting on an unrelated answer.

## Late feedback, repeated requests and continuation

Late feedback belongs to the session it addresses. Preserve it and update applicable prospective preparation through the remaining authorized wrap-up/save or an explicitly requested maintenance operation. During subsequent PLAY, it can remain in working conversation until the next authorized save; supplying it does not require a separate maintenance request. It does not reopen fictional outcomes or repeat session-end mechanics. If a later session is already active, retain its id and attach the late feedback to its original session; do not replace current Session continuity with the older one.

A repeated end request for an ended session completes only still-pending work or the requested persistence. An ordinary save after an ended session keeps that state. If the player resumes after closure, create the next session and carry unresolved required items forward with their original references. Reoffer unanswered feedback at a suitable opportunity without blocking requested PLAY or repeatedly asking it. Do not relabel unavailable feedback as declined.

If the player cancels an unfinished ending before any ending-boundary adjudication was settled, resume the same session and retain useful feedback. If that ending already produced an accepted adjudication, including a zero result or a ruling that the procedure is inapplicable, preserve that result and treat subsequent play as the next session; do not undo or repeat mechanics to change the boundary's meaning. A beginning procedure already settled for the active session is not itself evidence that its ending was settled.

## Persistence and evidence

Use INSTANCE/_SCHEMA.md for the Session continuity and PREP formats. The save procedure alone owns file publication. Include modified preparation and every affected mechanical/current owner in the same declared recovery write set; a retried write uses the retained target, not a fresh adjudication.

Full-save evidence may preserve actual operator feedback, its actual disposition, and session-boundary adjudication as clearly labelled OOC operational evidence. Keep this separate from fictional events and from private preparation. Preserve source gaps and the actual scope of any retained quotation. Checkpoints preserve the complete current state but create no new exact archive evidence; required unarchived feedback or pending inputs therefore remain at their current operational owner until faithfully preserved.

Normal autosave and scene handover preserve the current session and existing working preparation. They do not independently request feedback, complete a session, apply boundary mechanics, or author new preparation while serializing it. Existing exposure limits apply to private notes; this procedure promises no concealed storage.

## Save-review handoff

Once authorized session-boundary work is actually recorded, invoke CLOSE_CONTRACT's shared policy once. `tiered` selects a bounded source-first review for this full save, not a separate second saving operation. Include actual feedback disposition, completed goals, unfinished decisions and once-only boundary-effect evidence. Silence is not received/declined feedback. Save and stop promptly with an honestly incomplete review when source, reviewer or budget is unavailable. Do not invent a later answer or repeat a reward while waiting for an audit. Historical source spot-checks are selected maintenance within the available budget, not obligatory full-campaign rereads at each ending.
