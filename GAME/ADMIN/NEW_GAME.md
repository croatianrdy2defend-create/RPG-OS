# SETUP — New Game

Use after a request to create a campaign. This is a draft conversation, not PLAY. Reuse supplied facts; ask one small cluster of useful questions at a time. The goal is a playable campaign the player understands and accepts, not a completed questionnaire.

## Protect existing work

Resolve `RECOVERY/ACTIVE.md`, if present, through `ADMIN/RECOVERY.md`. Read CURRENT_SAVE and make one bounded INSTANCE/ARCHIVE inventory. A bound run, prior campaign evidence, non-template overlays/registers, or unexplained files require a separate clean kit. Never reset or inherit an old campaign to make NEW GAME succeed. Older state/contract formats use `ADMIN/UPGRADE_V07.md`; a v0.7 five-section agreement missing the v0.7.1 named clauses uses focused `ADMIN/RECALIBRATE.md` without state migration.

Engine/module ids use ASCII letters, digits, `.`, `_`, `-`, begin with a letter or digit, and contain no separators or traversal. Propose a safe id from the accepted name; the player need not invent technical identifiers. Check the exact module destination is absent and both permitted engine paths for collisions before planning writes. Never overwrite an installed module through NEW GAME.

## Neutrality before worldbuilding

Before proposing or drafting world lore, inhabitants or an opening, read and apply the selection-and-portrayal neutrality rule in [LAW — Establish](../OS/LAW.md#establish). Reuse sufficient guidance already loaded. This applies at every selected depth, including the first Quick start proposal and later expansion.

Use supplied sources, the agreed premise and authorized invention to establish what exists and how common it is. Distinguish explicit OOC worldbuilding requests from the PC's in-world preferences. Examples, their repetition or ordering, and visual asset availability supply neither population weights nor a reason to favor particular categories, traits, roles or responses. Select subjects before choosing portrayal references. Preserve supported demographics, local variation and the stated scope of biological and cultural facts; add no equal-representation quotas.

Keep reusable rules, briefs and portrayal guidance general within their actual scope. Give particular groups or features extra detail only where a source, accepted premise or actual clarification need supports it; detail length establishes neither prevalence nor descriptive priority. Put optional worked examples in separately retrieved material. Before presenting the proposal, review newly authored material for unsupported emphasis or defaults and correct them while preserving source-supported distinctions.

## Choose a useful pace

Offer these once unless the request already establishes the desired depth:

- **Quick start:** apply the neutrality rule above before proposing a coherent minimal campaign, PC, and opening from the supplied idea. Ask only what materially blocks it, then show the compact proposal.
- **Guided:** develop the premise, agreement, world, character, and opening through short adaptive clusters.
- **Detailed:** let the player select domains and develop those in stages. Stop or defer optional depth whenever requested.

Quick, Guided and Detailed all include the standard beginning/resumption preparation and end-of-session feedback routine in `ADMIN/SESSION.md`. Setup depth does not select whether the GM performs these duties. No separate feedback interview is required.

These are setup preferences, not runtime modes. World depth, character detail, bookkeeping precision, and mechanics are independent. A quick campaign may use a complete tactical sheet; a detailed world may use Freeform. Do not repeat already answered depth questions.

## Campaign spirit

Discover what the player wants to make this campaign satisfying, and translate it into practical GM guidance. Reuse supplied preferences, memorable moments, references or desired challenges. When useful, ask what mattered about them; the same example can support different interpretations. In Quick start, propose a concise reading with the ordinary proposal. Guided and Detailed may explore it further at the chosen pace. Already answered, declined or deliberately open preferences need no extra interview; discovering priorities through play is valid. No particular genre, emotional register or kind of enjoyment is the default.

Distill the proposed priorities into a compact paragraph under **Campaign promise**. Connect each important priority to useful GM decisions about opportunities, attention, portrayal, pacing or continuing consequences. State what the GM should support in practice; a mood label alone may be insufficient. Clarify competing priorities only where they would materially change play. Put actual prose implications in Presentation and any additional authority in its existing agreement section, without duplicating the whole statement. Keep optional reference scenes outside the resident agreement with an exact route only when useful; label them as preference evidence, not campaign canon or templates to reproduce.

Show this interpretation in the same reviewable proposal. Distinguish the player's stated preference from the GM's inference and accept only what the player agrees to. A reference does not import its people, events, outcomes or population weights. No separate confirmation, sixth section, named clause, score or campaign file is required. Preserve accepted spirit in the bound agreement so a fresh GM can use it without the setup conversation or cold POLICY.

During play, these accepted priorities guide judgment among plausible choices within existing authority. They do not alter established facts, sources, resolution or player control, require every scene to serve every priority, or force an emotion or outcome. Preserve appropriate variety, quiet and genuine success. Use the existing session preparation and feedback routine to revisit their practical effect. A single enthusiastic response or PC action does not silently change the agreement; material revisions use RECALIBRATE. Existing campaigns keep their accepted priorities in their current wording, with no mandatory retrofit or invented spirit where none was agreed.

## Build the playable minimum

Follow the player's starting point rather than a fixed question order. Establish the following with as few exchanges as practical. Open `ADMIN/CAMPAIGN_BUILD.md` for selected world/system depth and `ADMIN/CHARACTER_BUILD.md` for character work.

When the selected depth includes recurring people or consequential hidden facts, CAMPAIGN_BUILD offers two small optional aids: actionable portrayal anchors and notes separating fixed truth, beliefs, lies, and possible evidence. Use only what the premise and accepted authoring/disclosure grants warrant. They fit existing bodies and the same compact proposal; do not add required files, a new interview round, startup reading, or fixed answers where the campaign deliberately leaves matters open.

1. **The experience.** Establish premise, played perspective, important activities, material inclusions/exclusions, and accepted play form. Sandboxes, assigned missions, keyed exploration, cinematic chapters, domestic life, and combinations can all fit. A-E labels are optional; the actual agreement controls play. Selecting the form and disclosing its structure are separate authorities. An agreed destination or fixed outcome is allowed within its stated scope; never present a fixed result as an open contest or use it to choose a reserved PC response.
2. **Limits and presentation.** Invite operator limits early, before sensitive drafting. Record accepted hard-no/fade sentences under SAFETY's canonical labels; no personal explanation is required. If no voice was supplied, propose clear concrete prose, direct NPC dialogue, enough orientation to act, and concise responses; accept or revise that proposal with the agreement. Propose dialogue/narration balance, guidance, and status display only at useful granularity.
3. **Rules.** Use the named engine or offer installed choices and the option to supply another. Inspect canonical `ENGINE/<id>.md` and `ENGINE/<id>/ENGINE.md` entries with matching id and `class: engine`; aliases are not engines. State resolution, values needed now, and creation support: `self-contained`, `operator-values-required`, or `no-mechanical-sheet`. Follow `ADMIN/ADD_ENGINE.md` for a new adapter. Check the selected engine's genuine beginning/end procedures and source routes, or its explicit absence of such procedures. Resolve material cadence/policy choices in this same proposal; an engine without session mechanics still uses preparation and feedback. Never silently substitute Freeform or invent missing rules.
4. **The world.** Establish original, historical, established-source, or hybrid basis; opening scope; and relevant reality, inhabitants, ordinary distribution/variation, and model-default pitfalls. Do not assume human bodies, individual personhood, Earth time, money, a calendar, gender categories, romance, or fantasy cosmology. Omit irrelevant dimensions. Record source era/edition/continuity and priority where needed, plus exact fidelity, guided invention, and deliberate unknowns. Separate source-governed population selection from subsequent portrayal; examples, assets and player preferences do not supply selection weights or equal-representation quotas. A setting title is not evidence of a specific fact.
5. **The PC.** Establish the controlled perspective, essential concept, accepted starting facts, and engine minimum. An individual, nonhuman being, collective, organization, or another viewpoint can work if decision ownership and mechanical representation are clear. Optional history and sheet fields may remain unspecified. A character description does not grant GM control.
6. **Authority and continuation.** Propose what the GM may introduce, which established processes it must advance, any delegated routine PC conduct, and reserved meaningful decisions. Default to lived continuity with compression of declared/delegated routine only. Optional hardcuts need a bounded grant identifying what continuity, time, or danger may be skipped while preserving reserved decisions. A due world event does not imply PC participation. Permission is never an incident quota. Include relevant causal preparation at begin/resume and consolidation, feedback and genuinely due engine procedures at an explicit ending. A session boundary is OOC and does not itself advance fictional time, change a world process or choose PC participation. Establish the Retcon choice with the other short clauses below.
7. **The opening.** Propose one oriented playable situation: place, relevant time/order, perceivable circumstances, accepted prior commitments, ongoing activity, and the next material decision. Quiet is valid; a requested active premise should offer something usable to engage with. Do not invent PC history, allegiance, feelings, first action, or consent. Show proposed prior PC facts separately for acceptance. Opening mechanics must be available before bind.

Use supplied or accessible source references and label gaps honestly. Request the needed fact/procedure, preserve an unknown, or propose a clear divergence. Copy every source/fidelity constraint that affects PLAY into the agreement/brief, or a real source-authority body with an exact contract/descriptor route. The brief advertises its domain, trigger, and capability only; never leave an operative constraint solely in cold POLICY. Adapters contain compact procedures and field requirements, not reconstructed books or bulk copyrighted tables. For random mechanics, establish an available real randomizer or player-provided results; no mandatory helper script is assumed.

When a module prescribes a guide for focused physical portrayal, advertise that guide's first-focus trigger through the brief's matching capability and verify its exact route before bind. The relevant guidance must be available before the first focused description covered by it, even when the brief already supplies compatible anatomical facts. Loaded guidance may be reused within its actual coverage; setup does not require reading every visual reference.

## Independent agents at the selected depth

Bind the [Initial Encounter Baseline](../OS/AGENT_STATE.md#initial-encounter-baseline) and its active-participation lifecycle in the same setup proposal. Before individually resolved participation, appraisal or initiative involving the PC, establish or recover five current fields: **Condition and mode; Priorities and constraints; Perception and appraisal; Attraction; Engagement stance**. Each applicable field needs a concrete value or precise supported limitation before dependent behavior. Supplied nature, control, capabilities, identity and relationships constrain generation. Routine service does not excuse applicable attraction. Mere focused observation of an unaware individual requires the applicable portrayal source, not a mental baseline. This adds no full biography, new interview or unconditional per-field die.

Select one compatible default generation procedure and only the entity exceptions needed for the opening. MODULE supplies nature, capacities, control and meaningful variation; ENGINE governs generation and resolution. Identify exact sources, admissible inputs, eligibility, dependencies, outcome meanings and real random-input method before a draw. The temporary baseline does not require generating enduring personality traits. Overall attraction can include aversion; meaningful subtypes may be resolved later without inferring trust, availability or participation. Missing required sources, insufficient perception, actual absence and supported inapplicability remain distinct. A duty or existing partner does not make applicable attraction irrelevant.

Prepare only opening individuals whose current participation or independently resolved appraisal/action requires state. Shared orders, conditions and coordinated group conduct may remain group state; shared conduct alone needs no per-member initialization, while separately resolved individual decisions require their separate bases. Inherit applicable group and individual facts without backdating new private feelings. Bind reuse through attention gaps, relevant-event updates, and genuine deactivation: discard inconsequential temporary state while retaining consequential facts and unresolved matters. A later incidental encounter may generate fresh temporary values within surviving facts; continuing encounters and fixed opening results are not retry draws.

State the retention limit briefly where relevant: authorized opening private facts can be recorded during bind; later unsaved hidden state has best-effort conversational retention until an authorized save or checkpoint. PLAY writes no private scratch records; agent establishment alone starts no automatic checkpoint. A separately selected whole-present autosave policy uses `ADMIN/AUTOSAVE.md`, not a new private-state layer. Check spoiler visibility only if the requested mode depends on it; a filename or collapsed tool result does not prove secrecy. Use the existing reviewed/unfixed fallback when private authoring cannot be kept unexposed.

## Prepare one reviewable proposal

Show the actual proposal concisely. Under accepted structural opacity, show the truthful selection/disclosure envelope rather than exposing its concealed choice or plot:

- The agreement's **Campaign promise; Player control; GM initiative; Time and transitions; Presentation** sections, including the five named clauses below, source expectations, and concrete grants. Do not use retired axis, creative-mandate, or review-mode fields.
- The Setting Brief's **World identity; What is ordinary; Available depth**. Distinguish brief-guided portrayal from existing cold authority.
- The PC, mechanical readiness/deferrals, opening, and meaningful unknowns.
- Selected optional systems and their initial status/current routes; any useful portrayal/investigation notes within existing bodies; any private-authoring envelope and real host disclosure limitations. Review private content only to the extent the accepted disclosure mode permits.
- The small file manifest and accepted limits, distinguishing operator and module origins.

Compile the accepted generation permission and scope into GM initiative, its actual source/procedure into an exact engine or compatible module route, and practical disclosure limits into Presentation. The proposal must bind a usable method for the five current fields and their lifecycle, not merely say "use baselines." Keep the existing five sections and named clauses; no stance enum, retention questionnaire or new command is required. A newly authored module's fixed opening results become its reusable T0 baseline; later LOAD reuses those results rather than drawing a new population. Existing-module variability follows LOAD's unresolved-state rule.

Include the session routine within the same existing agreement sections:

- **GM initiative:** recover relevant causes, commitments, consequences and explicit feedback at begin/resume; prepare useful continuation within the granted authority. At an explicit ending, consolidate developments, invite or reuse feedback, handle actually due engine procedures and preserve useful preparation.
- **Time and transitions:** one play session may span chats, saves and handovers. SAVE/bare CLOSE preserves progress; an explicit ending invokes SESSION and its full save. Neither boundary advances fictional time without an actual selected rule or accepted transition.
- **Presentation:** use a brief public recap when useful and a short end feedback invitation; the player may answer or decline. Preserve the actual disposition, use relevant feedback in subsequent preparation, and identify unresolved adjustments. Keep private preparation out of ordinary narration within real host visibility limits.

These are responsibilities within the five sections, not a sixth clause or three additional approvals. Feedback grants no new PC control, invented history, population weighting or silently accepted agreement change.

Read `MODULES/_CONTRACT.md`, `ENGINE/_CONTRACT.md`, and `INSTANCE/_SCHEMA.md` before finalizing. POLICY retains `## Voice` and `## Campaign defaults` as setup proposals. Accepted presentation goes in the run contract; accepted stronger module limits go in SAFETY. POLICY is cold during PLAY.

Ordinary explicit acceptance of the displayed proposal suffices; no magic phrase is required. Re-present only material revisions or unresolved choices. A request to begin can accept a complete unambiguous proposal, but cannot fill missing grants or values. Unaccepted alternatives remain drafts.

## Five short agreement clauses

Put each named clause exactly once as a plain or bullet line in its specified section, followed by substantive free text. Include these in the same compact proposal; do not add five mandatory interview questions. Defaults are proposals for new setup, never inferred acceptance in an existing campaign.

- **Campaign promise — `Play form:`** State the actual accepted form/promise, including mixed/custom arrangements or explicit delegation within a described envelope. Default to the form the operator accepts in the proposal. Do not label a directed campaign a sandbox to conceal its structure.
- **GM initiative — `Form selection:`** Default: the operator accepts the stated form; the GM has no additional selection authority. Optional delegation names the alternatives/scope the GM may choose. Selection authority alone does not grant nondisclosure.
- **Presentation — `Structure disclosure:`** Default: the general form is known, plot remains hidden, and ordinary play has no repeated structure lectures. Optional structural opacity explicitly permits withholding the selected form/structure within a bounded delegation. Private plot, a hidden antagonist, or “surprise me” does not grant opacity. The control record remains truthful: it may retain the accepted selection/disclosure envelope without revealing a permitted concealed choice. Review must not force that choice into the public proposal or metadata.
- **Time and transitions — `Cuts:`** Default: lived continuity, compression of declared/delegated routine, no cinematic jumps. An optional hardcut grant states what unfinished continuity, elapsed time, or danger may be skipped and any limits. It never invents reserved PC choices or unauthorized outcomes. A declared sequence and standing routine/cut delegation are distinct permissions.
- **Player control — `Retcon:`** Default: OOC rewind is available. Optional ironman/no-retcon preserves valid accepted outcomes even when disappointing. Stopping/ending play, changing depiction, and correcting genuine errors remain separate capabilities. Do not silently weaken ironman or relabel dissatisfaction as a continuity error.

If a choice would take play outside the accepted premise, handle that boundary OOC: return to the premise, recalibrate, or end. Within the agreed premise, established world consequences proceed. Never enforce campaign fit by inventing impossible physics or irresistible NPC commands. An accepted structured destination does not require every intervening PC choice to be predetermined.

## Commit and hand over

If the operator wants switching between GMs, include an optional local scene-handover grant in the same agreement proposal. A requested switch, or a GM unable to continue the scene, pauses before the next unresolved input and exports available conversation plus established GM state through `ADMIN/SCENE_HANDOVER.md`. This needs no additional mandatory interview round. The grant does not authorize invented PC choices or permit claiming unavailable history or private reasoning as captured state. The factual, non-graphic return format is a transfer requirement, not a new rule governing the receiving scene. Create no handover package during setup.

Use the shared bind procedure in `ADMIN/LOAD.md` with this accepted draft and its new-file manifest. It protects all writes through recovery, verifies the results, and publishes CURRENT_SAVE last. Do not write persistent campaign files during the open interview or split module creation and bind into unprotected operations.

Its verification includes one noncanonical rehearsal of the selected encounter method: a representative individual and, where applicable, a coordinated group. Exercise establishment through response using the actual source routes and selected method, checking all five current fields and the boundary between shared conduct and personal decisions. Reuse a matching rehearsal already completed during this setup; NEW GAME, CAMPAIGN_BUILD and LOAD do not each run another one. Keep synthetic people and events out of T0 and campaign history. Report actual observations and any unrun or inapplicable coverage honestly; a route check alone is not a behavioral rehearsal.

Create only MODULE, SETTING_BRIEF, POLICY, the PC baseline/bundle, T0_SAVE, and selected real capability bodies. Initialize the bound CURRENT_SAVE administrative `Session continuity` section to `none`, independently of T0. Leave INSTANCE/PREP.md absent at bind; no play-session id, award or retrospective is created by setup. No mandatory roster, empty encyclopedia, or opening interpretation is needed.

After verification, report campaign/save identity, meaningful omissions, and how to resume from `OS/AGENTS.md`. Recommend a fresh PLAY chat so rejected drafts are cold. If the player continues here, perform the normal boot reads and use only accepted bound material. After verified bind, the first actual PLAY continuation enters ADMIN/SESSION.md once; setup completion, a readiness check and opening a fresh chat do not themselves begin a play session.

## Optional announced autosave

When selected, include `ADMIN/AUTOSAVE.md`'s policy in the same compact agreement: permission/triggers in GM initiative, a one-response-ahead generic warning in Presentation, and unchanged fictional time plus outgoing-save-before-switch in Time and transitions. The standard selected cadence is 15 completed PLAY replies, with consequential-state/scene-boundary triggers and a 65% context warning only from actual host telemetry or an operator report. Keep the operator's delay/disable/manual-save rights. Absent acceptance remains off; do not add a required interview question, sixth named clause, script dependency or empty autosave file.

Bind finishes before any autosave cadence starts. Autosave later invokes the existing protected complete-present CHECKPOINT and preserves the previous archive evidence boundary. It is not a full CLOSE or a promise that unsaved hidden state survives a model/reasoning change. The outgoing context must save first; preserve full evidence or a proper handover before discarding source.
