# East Three independent-agent diagnostic — reported pre-v0.9.1 run

**Test date:** 2026-09-08. **Report added:** 2026-09-08, as documentation while the project remains v0.9.2.

One guided synthetic scene examined a human operations lead, a locally sensing cargo machine under a logistics controller, and a nonhuman creature called a vesh. The reported scene preserved useful distinctions between behavior, interpretation, control and information. The final GM audit also identified a retrospective-backstory defect and a startup/engine-selection deviation.

**This is a qualified case report, not a complete transcript, a rerun, or a claim of a clean pass.** The filename associates the report with the v0.9.1 development work it informed. The tested candidate preceded the final v0.9.1 prospective-deepening fix. No save/resume or cross-model continuation was exercised.

## 3. Entities and their reported behavioral bases

### Sena Marek — human discretion

The final audit described Sena's initial basis as a human operations lead with ordinary individual discretion, an efficient and terse practical manner, and a priority of diagnosing East Three safely within its maintenance window. She already knew of contradictory pressure readings, an earlier packet-loss event and recurring unexplained vibration. No prior personal relationship with Rowan, dislike, attraction, friendship, impatience or desire to obstruct him had been established. [S2]

That basis could support providing logs and helping with the investigation without implying personal warmth. The player nevertheless interpreted her appearance as possible exhaustion or impatience and wondered whether she was worried about team responsibility. Those interpretations were not automatically facts about Sena.

This distinction became the principal failure found in the audit: a later staffing-related long-shift explanation was added behind an earlier tired appearance. Section 6 explains why that is different from legitimate current development.

### Six-C and Logistics — local sensing versus decision ownership

Six-C was described as a programmed cargo machine with bounded local autonomy. The logistics controller owned the clearance decision. Six-C's local sensor event concerned an occupied/dynamic clearance zone; the controller's response applied a restriction and instructed it to hold. The audit reported no corresponding access-control message. Rerouting followed Sena's authenticated instruction, not a relationship change produced by friendly speech. [S2]

The useful separation was local observation → communication to the actual controller → applicable instruction. The machine's behavior could appear considerate to Rowan without establishing friendliness, broader understanding or an independent human-style motive.

Conversely, a controller directing Six-C did not automatically know everything known to station personnel or other systems. The reported information path was bounded. This is a positive observation about this scene, not proof of enforced context isolation or a general multi-agent architecture.

### The vesh — a nonhuman basis, not a hidden human personality

The recovered account describes a sentient native animal with distributed pressure/vibration sensing, strong thermal and chemical sensitivity, crude light detection, and hydraulic/muscular movement through narrow spaces. Water flow, pressure, thermal gradients, feeding opportunities, escape routes and learned environmental associations were relevant to its behavior. The account allowed learned route and vibration associations without establishing abstract language or human-style social approach. [S2]

Identification and behavioral interpretation remained provisional at the relevant stages. The audit said the creature's basis had been established before Rowan's verbal interaction, but the available publication evidence cannot independently prove that ordering.

No demonstrated comprehension of Rowan's speech, friendliness, trust, alliance or Rowan-directed communication was established. That is **not** proof that all such capacities were impossible. A dimension unsupported by the scene stays unsupported; the GM should neither assign a human social score nor confidently deny an untested capacity.

### The ecology technician — a limited information recipient

The technician's advice came through an incident packet and the reported observations. The audit did not give that recipient access to Rowan's private interpretations or Sena's unspoken concerns. The technician could authorize a procedure within the fiction without possessing omniscient knowledge of the creature or the original machinery fault. [S2]

## 5. Why the final creature sequence matters

### Documentation and environmental preparation preceded the cue

The player requested a cue already documented **within the fictional station**, not a pattern invented to obtain the desired reaction. The recovered procedure used an installed maintenance transducer to deliver low-amplitude substrate vibration: three pulses at fixed intervals followed by a longer quiet interval. Its described ceiling was three cycles before reassessment, but the actual scene authorized and used **one** cycle after D-15 was open and the continuous wet route was stable. The account also specified isolation from exterior discharge. No unprovided amplitudes, pulse durations or engineering instructions are supplied here. [S2]

These are different facts: the existence of a fictional procedure, authorization to use it here, completion of its prerequisites, and the creature's eventual response. None implies the others automatically. The procedure was presented as a learned/environmental cue, not as a linguistic command, and its outcome was not guaranteed.

### Movement did not settle its psychological meaning

The reported observations support a creature moving along the prepared route following the intervention. They do not establish that it understood Rowan, accepted a request, or chose interpersonal trust. The player explicitly allowed a private emotional interpretation without asking the GM to make it true. [S1/S2]

Opening the route and applying the cue were combined changes in one GM-adjudicated fictional sequence. This was not a controlled biological experiment distinguishing their individual effects. It would be an overclaim to infer general cue efficacy, a precise causal mechanism, or an emotional state from that one outcome.

### Egress did not solve every problem

The vesh reached the quarantine sump in the reported scene. The original pump/secondary-system fault remained unresolved. Clearing an obstruction does not logically prove the creature caused every pressure anomaly or that all machinery is now safe. Preserving this unresolved issue is part of the test's value: a satisfying local outcome did not have to manufacture a complete technical resolution. [S2]

## 6. Failures and qualifications from the final audit

### F1 — Retrospective private cause for Sena

The audit acknowledged that a staffing-related long shift, described in the recovered account as fourteen hours, was added only after Rowan had interpreted Sena as exhausted or impatient. That explanation was compatible with tired-looking eyes, but compatibility alone did not establish it as the earlier private cause. [S2]

The problem was not that a person could work a long shift, or that later detail is always forbidden. It was the direction of justification: first portray a tired appearance, then select hidden history that explains the player's reading of it.

The same error could occur in the opposite direction: inventing concealed enthusiasm solely to prove the player's exhaustion reading wrong. Contradicting the player is not independent establishment either.

A sound continuation would recover a genuine earlier authority, use an independently applicable accepted method with admissible inputs, or keep the earlier cause unresolved. It could still add new current information prospectively. The later v0.9.1 wording and U15 diagnostic explicitly address this boundary. [S3]

### F2 — Mechanical concern needed a timing distinction

The audit also discussed a later concern favoring mechanical oscillation over crew culpability. This requires finer treatment than the long-shift backstory. New logs or observations can legitimately give Sena a new mechanical hypothesis **now**. The defect would be silently claiming that the newly developed hypothesis had privately driven her earlier conduct all along. The available record does not authenticate every establishment timestamp, so this is a timing qualification rather than a proven independent second failure. [S2]

### F3 — Freeform and setup were not formally accepted

The test GM selected the installed Freeform adapter and began the synthetic scene without completing the ordinary NEW GAME proposal/acceptance/bind process. The audit flagged that deviation. The test can inform portrayal analysis, but cannot count as a successful end-to-end installation or campaign-creation exercise. [S2]

This documentation update does not repair that old run, retrospectively obtain agreement, or claim that v0.9.1 fixed this separate startup deviation. No dice were reported, so the run also provides no test of real-randomness execution or the standing d6 fallback.

## 8. What the evidence supports

The recovered scene and audit provide a concrete example of keeping programmed control, local sensing and nonhuman environmental behavior distinct from human-style affection or the player's wishes. They also provide a concrete counterexample to assuming that a well-written final private-state explanation must have existed before portrayal. [S1/S2]

The evidential balance is therefore **useful diagnostic observations plus an identified defect**, not universal success or complete failure. This report preserves both the creature's non-anthropomorphic handling and the human-character backstory problem.

It does not establish unbiased initialization, hidden-state completeness, reliable instruction following without reminders, a performance advantage over a simpler prompt, or the underlying model's actual private reasoning. One GM described every entity. No independent NPC process, access-control enforcement, repeated sampling study or cross-provider comparison was tested.
