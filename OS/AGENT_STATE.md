# Independent agent state — v0.9.0

Cold procedure for establishing an entity-appropriate behavioral basis: the existing minimal baseline, not a new record type. Use before the focused portrayal or consequential behavior it governs, or for a materially unresolved change or initiative opportunity. Do not load it at every startup or before every line of dialogue. Reuse sufficient established state. No mandatory agent vector, draw, dossier, new state owner or writing permission is introduced.

## Identify the actual decision

Separate what must be resolved now from what the player hopes will happen. Opportunity selection, establishment of an entity's basis and resolution of a particular attempt are different questions. Do not choose the scene's ending first. A meeting does not settle a negotiation; conversational interest does not settle commitment.

Independence from the player's desired outcome is not individual autonomy. A person, creature, machine or collective may act through individual discretion, instincts, programming, orders, binding or shared control. Some behavior needs no conscious deliberation. Establish what actually governs this entity, not a human personality for every actor or maximum freedom for every body. These distinctions do not transfer the player's reserved control of a PC to the GM.

## Recover sufficient authority

Follow the relevant current individual/system record, then any materially needed stable source. Use `OS/RETRIEVAL.md` if the route is unclear. Existing individual facts outrank group tendencies; hard capability constraints remain binding unless an established exception actually applies. A hard limit, a typical tendency and an individual fact are different authorities. Do not invent an exception or erase an established one to obtain a desired outcome.

The Setting Brief supports ordinary portrayal. Retrieve a narrow nature, species, culture, design, faction or control source only when it can materially change the decision. Appearance alone establishes neither a custom nor a capacity or incapacity. Reuse a sufficient retrieved basis instead of reopening group lore every turn. The MODULE supplies setting-specific nature and control; the ENGINE governs applicable resolution; INSTANCE owns later current state. The OS supplies this procedure, not universal species psychology.

Distinguish fixed private truth, unknown-to-PC information, an answer deliberately open until its trigger, an unauthored eligible detail, an inapplicable dimension and a missing required source. Not applicable is not unknown or a zero score awaiting improvement. An unread or broken route is not permission to regenerate an entity. A friendly label cannot replace exact promises, disagreements or qualifications already established.

## Nature and control

Use only the questions material to this entity and decision: what it can perceive and do; what selects or constrains its actions; its applicable drives, directives or tendencies; its present condition; available information; and what could change its response. These are reasoning prompts, not required fields, a universal taxonomy or a complete simulation. A sentient individual may need preferences; a sensor-driven device may need detection and authorization rules instead of feelings.

Locate the decision owner and any local discretion. A controlled body is not automatically a separate autonomous mind, while membership in an ordinary organization does not erase individual discretion. A shared objective does not imply shared knowledge: use only the established communication channels, scope and delays. A controller does not automatically know everything its members sense; a member does not inherit everything its controller knows. When control changes or a link fails, use the supported transition rather than inventing freedom, paralysis or a new personality.

Shared governing state stays with its selected controller/system owner. Retain only relevant local differences, such as location, damage, perceptions or delegated scope, with the individual. No duplicate control register, all-to-all relationship graph or cast-wide update is required.

## Establish a minimal baseline on direct attention

When the player singles out an individual for direct attention or interaction, or an NPC directly engages the PC, recover or establish its minimal basis before the first focused portrayal or resolution that depends on it. "I approach them" suffices without an explanation of purpose. Singling out is a GM preparation trigger: it adds no PC approach, speech or intention and does not make an entity notice unperceived attention.

Player attention is not the fictional cause of an entity's existence or initiative. An established order, relevant perception, due process or authorized initiative can also require its basis before consequential behavior. Background crowds need no individual preparation; do not initialize unrelated agents. Establishing state neither activates preparation nor grants a new event or write permission.

Establish eligible gaps from accepted world and individual facts, pre-encounter circumstances and the authorized generation method. Prior causal history involving the PC is legitimate input. The PC's unverified interpretation, an unperceived player wish, a proposed narrative role and a desired success or refusal are not evidence of private nature. Before choosing new facts, separate those inputs from the admissible basis.

Preserve actual observed actions and speech as constraints, but do not promote their perceived meaning into motive, disposition or capability. A brief answer establishes a brief answer, not dislike; non-attack does not establish benevolence. Separate private state, outward presentation, the PC's explicitly declared interpretation and the entity's own supported interpretation. An entity need not have every one of those layers. Do not invent a PC interpretation or require every outward appearance to conceal a contrary motive.

Keep the basis small and entity-appropriate. Relevant activity or function, a drive or directive, present condition, information or a practical constraint may suffice. Where useful, ask what governs its behavior without this new PC intervention; an ordinary ongoing activity is a valid answer. Include supported opportunities and positive aims, not just obstacles manufactured to demonstrate independence. A routine clerk needs no biography; a controlled organism needs no friendship inventory.

Distinguish pre-existing facts from a first impression or response caused by the encounter. Establish eligible facts before the portrayal or resolution they affect; never invent prior motives afterward to explain a selected result. Existing observations constrain eligible generation without uniquely determining every hidden cause. Preserve supported outcomes and unresolved dimensions rather than rewriting earlier behavior. Applicable ENGINE procedures still govern uncertainty; prose traits supply no automatic numerical modifiers. Do not resolve the same reception once in the baseline and again as a reaction roll.

## Establish eligible gaps

Determine fairness-sensitive facts before resolving the attempt they affect. Use the bound engine or an accepted compatible generation procedure. Fix admissible inputs, eligible scope, context, constraints and outcome meanings before drawing. A new optional method needs acceptance where not already authorized; the fallback below is an available convention, not a universal behavioral model. A genuine conflicting rules change follows the engine-change procedure.

When randomness is claimed, obtain actual randomizer or player-supplied input. Otherwise use the engine's authorized judgment or agree an available alternative. Record the method honestly. Token sampling is not a die roll. A seed without its algorithm, inputs and mapping is not sufficient provenance. Randomness does not remove bias from a question or table selected to fit the player's desired outcome.

Conditional or correlated generation may preserve coherence; distinct dimensions need not be statistically independent. Apply hard exclusions before sampling. Do not use a reaction check or fallback to create an unsupported capacity or an inapplicable relationship dimension. Never switch a table, reinterpret a result or reroll because an outcome is inconvenient. Revisiting the same entity does not create a fresh initialization; genuinely seeking another uses actual time and opportunities.

An authorized GM-created private fact can be established without the PC learning it or the player separately approving each secret. The accepted authoring scope still governs. Unselected possibilities and dramatic interpretations remain preparation or inference; neither becomes truth merely because it would explain the desired response.

## Portray and change

Resolve behavior from the entity's supported nature, actual control, applicable drives/directives, condition and available information. Use knowledge, beliefs or aims only where supported; do not assume human-style deliberation. An inference remains possibly mistaken. Do not leak an unperceived OOC wish or another record's secrets into the entity or its controller.

Expression need not disclose private state: courtesy can coexist with dislike, and cooperation can follow duty. A favorable evaluation by an entity need not produce a player-favorable consequence; valuing a challenging opponent need not make it avoid combat. Cooperation need not establish affection. Agency does not require refusal, agreement, personal growth or a hidden contradiction.

Meaningful interaction, new evidence, completed obligations, treatment, changed orders and other supported circumstances may alter state. Retain a short cause at the affected scope, not a psychological essay. A response may change current state without retroactively choosing what existed before the encounter. Repetition alone earns neither progress nor forced resistance. Preserve individual variation where supported without breaking hard constraints; do not make every member of a category identical or every member an exception.

Deepen only as actual developments require additional facts. A relevant unknown may be established prospectively under its procedure; do not invent a past motive to explain behavior already resolved. Additional dialogue does not compel a biography or a new initialization. Independent initiative, cooperation, resistance, obedience, predation and indifference are all possible where the established entity and situation support them; there is no outcome quota.

Preserve PC authorship and actual declarations. For entities where they apply, attraction, trust, cooperation, commitment and present voluntary participation are not interchangeable. Ordinary declared conduct needs no extra permission ritual; an unchosen commitment remains with its author. Follow the accepted campaign's presentation and operator limits.

## Resolve operative opportunities

Resolve relevant open matters when current activity, circumstances or a declared wait makes their outcome material. Use existing state, available channels and actual constraints; a missing motive or plan does not establish inaction. Sparse state needs no dossier before the accepted fallback can resolve an eligible unknown. Unrelated agents need no check. Initiative still requires the applicable cause, procedure or accepted grant.

Keep the question and interval bounded. An earlier reaction retains its original scope. If an event ends a wait, place it within supported circumstances and stop there; leave later time uncommitted. Retain the question/window, method and result in the existing individual/system owner when needed at the next requested save. Repeated queries or subdivisions create no extra draws for the same resolved opportunity. Only an actual operative condition needs an Active processes cue; no automatic timer is added.

## Fallback oracle for eligible unknowns

Use when the player requests this random fallback or accepts it as a standing method. Facts and applicable ENGINE procedures take priority; grounded judgment remains sufficient where supported. Otherwise resolve the eligible uncertainty directly:

1. Frame one concrete yes/no question and its scope before drawing, such as whether a contact sends a message within this week. Keep impossible outcomes out; preserve fixed facts and PC authorship.
2. Use the already accepted oracle, or this fallback convention: one actual d6, **1–3 No; 4–6 Yes**. These even odds are a game convention, not a claim about real-world probabilities. A standing selection covers subsequent questions without new permission each time. If real randomness is unavailable, obtain the player's roll or agree an available alternative.
3. Record the question, mapping, actual input and scoped result. Portray that result without inventing prior motives or history to justify it. Resolve further consequences through their actual circumstances and applicable rules; a secret being disclosed does not automatically mean every enemy learns it or the worst outcome occurs.

Importance to the campaign does not exempt an eligible uncertain outcome from the accepted method. This fallback does not replace missing required rules/values, regenerate inaccessible established state, or open a deliberately unfixed fact before its trigger. It creates no per-agent biography, automatic recurring roll or PLAY write. An omitted resolution stays an audit gap until an accepted correction; do not fabricate retrospective dice or reasons.

## Retain, save and transfer honestly

Retain a compact factual basis: relevant governing state and constraints, meaningful change causes, unresolved dimensions and any actual generation method/result reference. PEOPLE owns individual state; a collective/controller may use its existing NOW owner. Shared state has one authority, not a copy per body. Direct attention and a tiny baseline do not automatically require a durable record. Promote by continuing causal significance under the existing save rules. No separate stance register is created.

PLAY remains read-only. Unsaved private facts have best-effort retention in available context, not an independently recoverable hidden commitment. A claimed private determination is not proof of independent generation. Store established fictional facts and method provenance, not internal reasoning. A requested Save or CHECKPOINT uses the complete-present and recovery procedure in `ADMIN/CLOSE_CONTRACT.md`; a checkpoint keeps its prior archive evidence boundary. This protocol initiates no automatic establishment checkpoint, background save or private scratch file.

Handover exports actual available state and source gaps, never internal reasoning. The receiving GM leaves source authorities frozen; newly developed recipient-side state has only the retention actually available until return. Import accepted changes once through `ADMIN/SCENE_HANDOVER.md`.

Adoption is preservation-first: do not regenerate existing individuals, controllers, relationships or history. Recover their established basis and fill only eligible gaps. A disputed established fact uses `ADMIN/CORRECT.md`; changed authoring methods or permissions require their accepted prospective procedure. Relabeling a prior fact as an impression is not a silent repair.

If consequential state is unavailable, seek its source and preserve supported outcomes. Identify the narrow missing basis rather than selecting a convenient replacement or claiming to reconstruct a lost draw. Pause only a decision materially dependent on it. Saving, review and resumption never supply a missing cause or reroll authority.
